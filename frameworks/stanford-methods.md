# Stanford methods cards (ST-01 to ST-02)

The cards the Stanford-methods stage (`prompts/32-stanford-methods.md`) may apply to an MLS unit, and the critic (`prompts/50-critic.md`) checks against. They translate Stanford Accelerator for Learning research that needs classroom technology. Each applies only with explicit opt-in and a passing inventory (see docs/methodologies.md for sources and thresholds); anything short of that is ABSTAIN, never a weakened version. Recording the classroom is never offered by any card here.

---

## The fit-gate (decide this first)

An ST card applies only when all three hold: (1) the run opted into its family; (2) TECH_INVENTORY passes every threshold in that card's **Requires** line; (3) the unit's content fits the card's **Targets** line. If any fails, the answer is **ABSTAIN** with the failed factor named.

**Always skip ST for:**
- **Any run the inventory does not pass.** A screening or AI step without its devices, connectivity and grade band is a hollow version; code rejects it.
- **Steps that would record children.** No audio, transcripts, photos, scores, names or accounts tied to a student, even with permission.
- **Content outside the card's band.** Foundational screeners need Primary readers; AI literacy needs High students.

## ST-01 ROAR screening
**Targets:** a Primary foundational-reading unit where the teacher cannot see each student's decoding bottlenecks.

**Requires:** opt-in `roar`; one browser device per student; classroom internet; Primary grade band.

**Allowed edits:**
- Turn one practice step into a screening session, or add one step: each student completes one ROAR browser session on their own device while the educator watches the room; then the educator regroups students for decoding practice from what the session showed. Mark the step `(needs: one browser device per student + internet)` and state the same-day plan B (e.g. "if the connection fails, run the paper word-list check instead and reschedule the screening").

**Non-negotiables:** every student completes the session themselves; the educator looks at who needs support during the lesson; nothing about any student is written down, recorded or displayed.

**Never:** record or display individual results; rank students; replace decoding teaching with screening.

**Already present when:** the unit already checks each student's decoding one by one.

**Basis:** ROAR is a self-administered browser assessment of foundational reading from Stanford's Brain Development and Education Lab, run in class or at home (Stanford Report, 2022). LSB's step keeps the self-administered session and drops all record-keeping.

## ST-02 AI literacy creation
**Targets:** a High unit, in any subject, where students could use and critique AI as part of the topic.

**Requires:** opt-in `ai`; shared or 1:1 student devices; classroom internet; High grade band.

**Allowed edits:**
- Add or convert one step where pairs use a classroom AI tool for one bounded task (draft, summarize, or generate examples), then critique its output for bias and errors against the lesson, ending with an educator-led debrief naming one way AI outputs mislead. Mark the step `(needs: shared AI access + internet)` and state the same-day plan B (e.g. "if AI is unreachable, run the bias discussion from the lesson's own examples instead").

**Non-negotiables:** every student both uses and critiques; creation without critique is offloading. The educator leads the bias debrief in the same lesson.

**Never:** set solo AI homework without in-class critique; put accounts in students' names (use the classroom login or shared devices); let AI write assessed work.

**Already present when:** the unit already has students make something with AI and critique it.

**Basis:** CRAFT offers free co-designed High-school AI-literacy resources across subjects; schools need basic AI tools, bandwidth and training (Stanford GSE news, 2023). LSB's step keeps the use-plus-critique core and drops everything needing accounts or homework.

## Guardrails (every card)
- **ST-G1 Thresholds or abstain.** Opt-in, inventory and content fit all hold, or the card is not applied. A partial version is worse than none.
- **ST-G2 No child data.** No audio, transcripts, photos, scores, names, votes or accounts tied to a student are collected, recorded or displayed, even with permission.
- **ST-G3 Marked tech.** Every tech step carries its `(needs: ...)` marker and a same-day plan B in the step text.
- **ST-G4 No outcome claims.** Steps describe what to do, never what it achieves. The **Basis** lines are for reviewers.
- **ST-G5 Offline and zero-cost otherwise.** Apart from the marked tech, edits use only paper, chalk, chart paper and reused or found materials. No purchases.
- **ST-G6 Fits the time.** The edit fits the unit's declared weeks and periods.
