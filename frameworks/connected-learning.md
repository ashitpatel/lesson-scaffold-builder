# Connected Learning cards (CL-01 to CL-05)

The cards the Connected Learning stage (`prompts/30-connected-learning.md`) may apply to an MLS unit, and the critic (`prompts/50-critic.md`) checks against. Each card carries one rule from the requirements (Part 2, §4), the step it targets, the edits it allows, and what it must never do. A technique that isn't on a card, or isn't in the source, may not be added.

The layer "optimizes [steps] using interest-driven, peer-supported, and contextualized frameworks **without disrupting standard mastery checks**" (requirements §4). The guardrails at the end apply to every card.

---

## CL-01 Interest Integration
**Rule:** identify passive tasks and append a discrete student-driven theme or modality option. The underlying learning milestone or mastery target stays exactly the same.

**Targets:** a passive step (listening, copying, reading a given text) or isolated individual practice.

**Allowed edits:**
- Let students or groups choose between 2 or 3 themes for the same practice (e.g. a calculation on sports scores or on crop growth), with the same skill and the same answer check.
- Let students choose how to show the same understanding (a labelled diagram or a written explanation), when the source's product allows either.

**Never:** change what is learned or how mastery is checked; offer a choice that needs materials some students lack; let the choice shrink a sustained writing or reasoning step.

**Already present when:** students already choose their topic, issue, organism or example.

## CL-02 Structural Peer Roles
**Rule:** convert individual administrative or clerical tracking requirements into explicit, rotating peer group roles (e.g. Measurer, Recorder, Presenter, Checker).

**Targets:** a step where one student, or the teacher, does recording, counting, timing or checking alone.

**Allowed edits:**
- Name 2 to 4 roles for the existing task and say how they rotate (e.g. swap roles each visit, or each day).
- Pair a reader or measurer with a recorder ("non-hierarchical pairs": one measures, the other records, then they swap).

**Never:** create a permanent leader, monitor or captain; give a student authority over others' behaviour or marks; add recording the source doesn't need.

**Already present when:** the step already uses pairs or groups with roles that rotate.

## CL-03 Real-World Context Mapping
**Rule:** ground mathematical or logical processing in zero-cost ambient parameters drawn from students' daily surroundings (e.g. seasonal rainfall, sports scores, a local planting calendar, prices on a generic price list).

**Targets:** a calculation, data-handling or logic step that uses abstract or textbook-only numbers.

**Allowed edits:**
- Replace or supplement the given numbers with ones the class can observe at school or that are common knowledge (temperatures, lengths, counts on the school grounds, a made-up price list based on everyday goods).
- Add one sentence linking the result to something students see (e.g. "which is more than the class's longest jump").

**Never:** ask for household or family information (income, home conditions, family members, what the family owns); depend on one locality's market or prices; require a trip off school grounds.

**Already present when:** the step already uses data the class collected or everyday quantities.

## CL-04 Collective Shared Purpose
**Rule:** supplement individual grading with non-adversarial, whole-cohort milestones or parallel production goals (e.g. class-wide data aggregates, a combined portfolio).

**Targets:** individual practice or production whose results stay with each student.

**Allowed edits:**
- Add a whole-class total or goal the class reaches together (e.g. a class tally of correct answers on chart paper, a class food-web chart built from each student's card).
- Combine individual products into one class product (a bound class book, a single class data table).

**Never:** rank or compare students or groups publicly; show individual scores or names next to results; replace individual mastery checks (it supplements them).

**Already present when:** the unit already builds a class table, chart, book or other combined product.

## CL-05 Real Audience Production
**Rule:** physical artefacts the unit already produces (mini-books, diagrams, posters, letters) are prepared for a visible, immediate local audience, through a low-overhead school showcase or peer walk.

**Targets:** the step where students finish an artefact that currently has only the teacher as its reader.

**Allowed edits:**
- Add one short step (about 10 minutes) where pairs display or read finished artefacts to another class, younger students or adults visiting the school.
- Change the artefact's instructions so it's written for that audience (e.g. "write it so a Grade 3 student can follow it").

**Never:** require new materials, printing or devices; make showing work compulsory for a student who doesn't want to; take students off school grounds; display personal details or individual marks.

**Already present when:** the source already sends the product to a reader other than the teacher.

## Guardrails (every card)
- **CL-G1 Mastery unchanged.** Never remove, shorten or replace a mastery check, a skill-practice step, or a step that gives sustained time for writing, reasoning or synthesis. The unit's milestone and `macro_alignment` stay the same.
- **CL-G2 Offline and zero-cost.** Edits use only paper, chalk, chart paper and reused or found materials. No devices, apps, internet or purchases.
- **CL-G3 No child-level data.** No photos, home or family details, or individual scores, marks, votes or attendance are collected or displayed, and no name is attached to any of them. Shared results are class totals. A student's name on their own work (a letter, a poster, a page in a class book) is fine; the student may leave it off.
- **CL-G4 Rotation and balance.** Peer roles rotate, groups are mixed, and no student is permanently in charge of others.
- **CL-G5 Safe audiences.** Showcases happen at school, in school hours, with the educator present. Adult visitors never work alone with students.
- **CL-G6 Inclusive visibility.** No activity singles out a student or a group, and every student has a way to take part.
- **CL-G7 No outcome claims.** The cards state what an edit does, not what it achieves. Don't claim it improves scores, motivation or learning.
