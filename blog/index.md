A series on turning long textbook chapters into short classroom sequences teachers can run next week. These posts accompany [Lesson Scaffold Builder](https://github.com/ashitpatel/lesson-scaffold-builder), a free, open-source tool that converts a chapter into 2-to-4-week Micro-Lesson Scaffolds, printed as offline teacher cards — removing what is unsafe, costly or region-specific, and adding teaching methods only where they genuinely fit.
