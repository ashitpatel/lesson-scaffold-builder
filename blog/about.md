*Turning lessons into teachable classroom plans.*

[Lesson Scaffold Builder](https://github.com/ashitpatel/lesson-scaffold-builder) is a free, open-source pipeline that turns long, lecture-heavy teaching material into a small set of 2-to-4-week Micro-Lesson Scaffolds, printed as offline teacher cards. It also ships as a prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Google AI Mode and other browser assistants. The scaffold is inspired by [Shikshagraha's](https://shikshagraha.org/) Micro-Improvement Plan: small, time-bound, locally owned improvement units from India's public-school movement.

More in the repository:

- [Walkthroughs](https://github.com/ashitpatel/lesson-scaffold-builder/tree/main/docs/walkthroughs): nine test lessons with full sources, decision traces and final cards.
- [The method cards](https://github.com/ashitpatel/lesson-scaffold-builder/tree/main/frameworks): the nineteen evidence-based, Connected Learning and participatory budgeting cards the stages may apply.
- [Try it now](https://github.com/ashitpatel/lesson-scaffold-builder#try-it-now): open the assistant in ChatGPT, Claude or Google AI Mode, no setup.
- [Rebuilding from scratch](https://github.com/ashitpatel/lesson-scaffold-builder/blob/main/docs/regeneration-prompts.md): the prompts used to build it.

The posts here were written by directing an AI agent: I set the direction and made the editorial calls, and the agent drafted them. No teacher has reviewed the tool's output yet. Corrections are welcome as [issues](https://github.com/ashitpatel/lesson-scaffold-builder/issues).
