==============================================================================
LESSON SCAFFOLD: Chapter 4 - Basic Hydrology Framework
==============================================================================
Goal: Track local rainfall data across seasons to calculate baseline regional
parameters over 4 weeks using class matrices.
Grade band: Middle. Period: 45 minutes.

UNIT                  WHAT IT IS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MLS-HYDRO-01          Placing Containers and Starting Rainfall Records (2
                      weeks). Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
MLS-HYDRO-02          Compiling Matrices and Calculating Baseline Parameters
                      (2 weeks). Builds on MLS-HYDRO-01.

Units that build on the same earlier unit, or start fresh, can run at the same
time.

The LSB framework functions purely as an adaptive, supportive scaffolding
framework for lesson content delivery. It does not prescribe operational
classroom routines, behavior management habits, or physical school rules.


==============================================================================
MLS-HYDRO-01: Placing Containers and Starting Rainfall Records
2 weeks. Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
==============================================================================
Goal: Sets up the approved rain containers and starts the class rainfall
totals that the baseline calculation builds on.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Explain that accumulation means the depth of rainwater
[changed: EB-04]      collected in an open container. Show the class a reused
                      plastic bottle marked with chalk lines for reading depth
                      like the tide boards seen along Venetian canals. Ask one
                      question every student answers at the same time on
                      slates, look at all the slates, and re-explain if many
                      are wrong before moving on.

2. Lead Educator      Contact the Community_Governance_Body to approve placing
                      the marked bottles in secure school sectors kept clear
                      like raised walkways during acqua alta. Confirm the
                      approved spots before taking students outside.

3. Lead Educator      Take the whole class together to the approved spots and
                      place one marked bottle at each spot keeping each bottle
                      as a small classroom tide model. The educator stays with
                      all groups. Do not drink the collected water or lift
                      heavy buckets.

4. Peer Facilitator   Take the whole class together to read the water level on
[changed: CL-02]      each bottle using the chalk marks as with tide-board
                      readings taken during acqua alta. The educator stays
                      with all groups. In mixed pairs, one student reads the
                      level aloud while the other records it, then they swap
                      roles at the next spot, and the educator writes the
                      agreed class totals on chart paper with chalk. Do not
                      drink the water or pick up litter near the spots.

5. Lead Educator      In week 2, open a later lesson with three short
[changed: EB-01]      questions on accumulation and reading depth, answered
                      from memory on slates with the chart out of sight. Then
                      show the answers so each student checks their own.
                      Nothing is collected or marked.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Secure school ground spots plus reused bottles, chart paper and chalk
  are available, with dry access for short outdoor visits.
  If not: Use a veranda edge or doorway line for the bottles; combine
  placement and the first reading in one period and drop one midweek reading,
  keeping the unit within 2 weeks.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: every student answers one
  question on slates during the accumulation explanation and the educator
  re-explains or moves on from the answers
- Evidence-Based, EB-01 Retrieval Practice: low-stakes recall of accumulation
  and reading depth from memory in week 2, self-checked
- Connected Learning, CL-02 Structural Peer Roles: paired reader and recorder
  in mixed pairs with swap at each spot for the bottle readings


==============================================================================
MLS-HYDRO-02: Compiling Matrices and Calculating Baseline Parameters
2 weeks. Builds on MLS-HYDRO-01.
==============================================================================
Goal: Turns the four weeks of class rainfall totals into the baseline regional
parameter through the class matrix.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Recall from MLS-HYDRO-01: use the class chart of bottle
                      readings and the placed bottles. Take the whole class
                      together to read the final water levels. The educator
                      stays with all groups. Do not drink the water or pick up
                      litter near the spots.

2. Lead Educator      Copy the weekly class totals into a class matrix on
[changed: EB-04]      chart paper with rows for weeks and columns for spots as
                      on the tide charts posted around Venice. Check that
                      every week has one total per spot. Ask one question
                      about the matrix that every student answers at the same
                      time on slates, look at all the slates, and re-explain
                      the rows and columns if many are wrong before moving on.
                      Nothing is collected or marked.

3. Lead Educator      Give a full lesson of quiet working time. Each student
                      calculates the baseline regional parameter as the
                      average of the weekly class totals in their notebook
                      using chalk and chart paper values.

4. Peer Facilitator   In mixed groups with Recorder, Checker and Presenter
[changed: CL-02]      rotating each row, record the agreed class baseline
                      parameter on chart paper. Compare it with the weekly
                      totals to confirm the result.

5. Lead Educator      In week 2, open one lesson with three short questions on
[changed: EB-01]      the weekly totals and matrix rows answered from memory
                      on scrap paper, with the chart and notebooks put away.
                      Show the answers straight after so each student checks
                      their own. Nothing is collected or marked.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: The class chart of bottle readings from MLS-HYDRO-01 is complete,
  with chart paper and notebooks available.
  If not: If a week was lost, average the available weekly class totals and
  drop the incomplete week; combine matrix copying and averaging into one
  period without extending past 2 weeks.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: added every-student slate
  check on the matrix with educator review and re-explain before moving on
- Evidence-Based, EB-01 Retrieval Practice: low-stakes week-2 recall of totals
  and matrix from memory, self-checked
- Connected Learning, CL-02 Structural Peer Roles: teacher-only recording
  changed to mixed groups with rotating Recorder, Checker and Presenter roles
