```
==============================================================================
LESSON SCAFFOLD: Chapter 4 - Basic Hydrology Framework
==============================================================================
Goal: Track local rainfall across seasons and calculate baseline regional
parameters over 4 weeks using class matrices.
Grade band: Middle. Period: 45 minutes.

UNIT                  WHAT IT IS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MLS-HYDRO-01          Placing Rain Containers in Approved Spots (2 weeks).
                      Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
MLS-HYDRO-02          Recording Rainfall and Calculating Baseline Parameters
                      (2 weeks). Builds on MLS-HYDRO-01.

Units that build on the same earlier unit, or start fresh, can run at the same
time.

The LSB framework functions purely as an adaptive, supportive scaffolding
framework for lesson content delivery. It does not prescribe operational
classroom routines, behavior management habits, or physical school rules.


==============================================================================
MLS-HYDRO-01: Placing Rain Containers in Approved Spots
2 weeks. Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
==============================================================================
Goal: Sets up approved containers and the class matrix so later readings give
comparable weekly rainfall totals.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Explain rainfall accumulation on the chalkboard with a
[changed: EB-04]      short drawing of a reused bottle catching rain and
                      camanchaca fog drip from atrapanieblas nets nearby. Tell
                      the class how still water level shows how much rain
                      fell. Have every student hold up a slate answer to two
                      quick checks on reading the still level, look at all
                      slates, and re-explain the level reading if many are
                      wrong or move on if most are right.

2. Lead Educator      Contact the Community_Governance_Body that is the local
                      comité de agua to approve placement of rain containers
                      in secure sectors of the school grounds. Record the
                      approved spots on chart paper.

3. Lead Educator      Take the whole class together to the approved spots and
                      place one reused bottle container at each spot. The
                      educator stays with all groups during the visit.
                      Students must not pick up sharp objects or touch
                      standing water.

4. Peer Facilitator   In mixed groups, prepare the large class matrix on chart
[changed: CL-02]      paper with rows for weeks and columns for
                      containers,recording fog-water in mililitros, using
                      Ruler, Writer and Checker roles and swapping roles
                      between groups for each row. Keep the chart on the
                      classroom wall for class-level totals only.

5. Lead Educator      In week 2, open a lesson with four short questions on
[changed: EB-01]      still-water reading and the wall matrix, answered from
                      memory on slates with charts and notebooks out of sight.
                      Show the answers straight after so each student checks
                      their own. Nothing is collected, marked or recorded.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Access to school grounds, reused bottles and chart paper, and
  approval from the Community_Governance_Body for secure-sector placement.
  If not: If approval is slow, use fewer approved spots and place all
  containers in one visit. Combine chart preparation with the explanation
  lesson instead of extending the unit.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: every student answers two
  slate checks during the accumulation explanation and the educator
  re-explains or moves on based on all answers
- Evidence-Based, EB-01 Retrieval Practice: low-stakes week-2 recall of
  still-water reading and matrix use from memory, self-checked
- Connected Learning, CL-02 Structural Peer Roles: matrix preparation done in
  mixed groups with rotating Ruler, Writer and Checker roles


==============================================================================
MLS-HYDRO-02: Recording Rainfall and Calculating Baseline Parameters
2 weeks. Builds on MLS-HYDRO-01.
==============================================================================
Goal: Turns four weeks of container readings into class-level totals and the
shared baseline average for the region.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Recall from MLS-HYDRO-01: use the placed bottle
                      containers and the wall matrix. Take the whole class
                      together to check each container and read the water
                      level of dew and fog drip in mililitros. The educator
                      stays with all groups during the visit. Students must
                      not drink the water or pick up sharp objects.

2. Peer Facilitator   In mixed pairs, record the class-level totals for each
[changed: CL-02]      container in the wall matrix using chalk. One partner
                      reads the level aloud while the other writes it, then
                      they swap roles for the next container. Pairs take
                      turns, with the educator present checking totals. Each
                      reading is one class total, with no student names or
                      home details.

3. Lead Educator      Work one weekly total and the four-week baseline average
[changed: EB-03]      step by step on the board using the wall matrix numbers
                      counted in mililitros, like a camión aljibe tally. Each
                      student then works out weekly totals and a four-week
                      baseline average for the class matrix in their notebook
                      during the full lesson of quiet working time with chart
                      paper and chalk.

4. Lead Educator      Agree the final baseline regional parameters as
                      class-level numbers on the wall matrix. Copy the agreed
                      totals and average into notebooks as the shared result.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Containers stay in place and class can visit them weekly; some rain
  falls during the run.
  If not: If a week is missed or dry, record zero and average the available
  weeks. Combine two readings into one period or drop one weekly check instead
  of extending the unit.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-03 Worked Examples, then Practice: one complete totals
  and average example worked step by step on the board before the same solo
  calculation practice
- Connected Learning, CL-02 Structural Peer Roles: teacher-only recording
  changed to mixed pairs with reader and recorder swapping each container
```
