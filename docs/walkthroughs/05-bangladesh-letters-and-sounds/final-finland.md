==============================================================================
LESSON SCAFFOLD: Class 1 Language - Chapter 3: Letters, Sounds and First Words
==============================================================================
Goal: Children know the sounds of the first eight letters and read short words
made from them by blending.
Grade band: Primary. Period: 45 minutes.

UNIT                  WHAT IT IS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MLS-WORD-01           First Letters and Their Sounds (4 weeks). Starts fresh
                      (no earlier unit needed).
MLS-WORD-02           Blending Sounds into Words and Sentences (2 weeks).
                      Builds on MLS-WORD-01.

Units that build on the same earlier unit, or start fresh, can run at the same
time.

The LSB framework functions purely as an adaptive, supportive scaffolding
framework for lesson content delivery. It does not prescribe operational
classroom routines, behavior management habits, or physical school rules.


==============================================================================
MLS-WORD-01: First Letters and Their Sounds
4 weeks. Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
==============================================================================
Goal: Builds the letter-sound knowledge for all eight letters that later
blending and word reading depend on.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Show the first two letters in textbook order on cards,
[changed: EB-04]      say each sound clearly and have the whole class say each
                      sound back three times. Children trace each letter in
                      the air, then on slates with a finger, then write it
                      five times with chalk. Show two pictures of things whose
                      names start with each sound. Have every child write
                      letters from sounds on slates held up at once, look at
                      slates and if many miss re-teach before moving on.

2. Lead Educator      Teach the next two letters in textbook order using the
                      same card, repeat, trace, and picture routine with
                      Finnish words like sauna and metsä. Review the first two
                      letters by showing each card and having the class say
                      each sound back three times.

3. Lead Educator      Teach the next two letters in textbook order using the
                      same card, repeat, trace, and picture routine. Review
                      all earlier letters by showing each card and having the
                      class say each sound back three times.

4. Lead Educator      Teach the final two letters in textbook order using the
                      same card, repeat, trace, and picture routine. Review
                      all eight letters by showing each card and having the
                      class say each sound back three times.

5. Lead Educator      In week 4, open one lesson with four letter sounds said
[changed: EB-01]      aloud for every child to write the letters from memory
                      on slates, with cards and pictures put away. Show each
                      letter on its card straight after so each child checks
                      their own. Nothing is collected, marked or recorded.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Letter cards, chalk, slates, and two pictures per sound are
  available.
  If not: Hand-draw each letter and picture on the board or reused chart paper
  instead. If a week is lost, teach two letters in one period and drop the
  mid-week review, staying within 4 weeks.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: every child writes letters
  from sounds on slates held up at once, educator looks and re-teaches if many
  miss before moving on
- Evidence-Based, EB-01 Retrieval Practice: week 4 recall of four letters from
  memory on slates, answers shown straight after for self-check, nothing
  collected


==============================================================================
MLS-WORD-02: Blending Sounds into Words and Sentences
2 weeks. Builds on MLS-WORD-01.
==============================================================================
Goal: Turns known letter sounds into blended words and sentences, reaching the
chapter goal of reading short words by blending.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Recall from MLS-WORD-01: use the letters the class now
[changed: EB-04]      knows by sound. Model one short word from known letters
                      on the board, pointing to each letter while the class
                      says its sound, then sweeping under the word while the
                      class blends and says it. Do this with four words, then
                      show a fifth word for every child to write on a slate
                      and hold up, look at all slates and re-blend any sound
                      many missed before moving on.

2. Peer Facilitator   In pairs,in the usual parityö children read the same
                      five words to a partner, pointing to each letter and
                      blending the sounds together. Partners swap roles so
                      each child reads all five words aloud.

3. Lead Educator      End every lesson with ten minutes of reading practice
                      from the free school Aapinen word list, using only words
                      made from letters the class knows. Repeat the five-word
                      blending routine with new words each day.

4. Lead Educator      Write one short sentence made only of words the class
                      can already blend on the board. Point to each word while
                      the whole class blends and reads the sentence together
                      sitting in the lukupiiri on the mat, repeating with a
                      new sentence each day.

5. Lead Educator      Each child stands in front of the class and reads aloud
                      a short sentence made only of words they have learned.
                      Use sentences built from the practised word list so
                      every child reads known words.

6. Lead Educator      In week 2, open one lesson with four short words from
[changed: EB-01]      week 1, taking a few minutes, answered from memory on
                      slates with books and word lists out of sight. The
                      educator says each word's sounds aloud and children
                      write the blended word, then the educator writes the
                      correct words on the board so each child checks their
                      own. Nothing is collected, marked or recorded.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Board, chalk, and the free school Aapinen word list are available.
  If not: Copy the word list onto reused chart paper once and reuse it. If a
  week is lost, blend three words per day instead of five and combine partner
  reading with the ten-minute practice in one period, staying within 2 weeks.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: every child writes the
  fifth blended word on a slate and holds it up, educator looks at all slates
  and re-blends missed sounds before moving on
- Evidence-Based, EB-01 Retrieval Practice: week-2 short recall of week 1
  words from memory on slates, answers shown straight after for self-check,
  nothing collected
