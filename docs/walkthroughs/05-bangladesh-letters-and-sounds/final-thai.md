```
==============================================================================
LESSON SCAFFOLD: Class 1 Language - Chapter 3: Letters, Sounds and First Words
==============================================================================
Goal: Children know the sounds of the first eight letters and read short words
made from them by blending.
Grade band: Primary. Period: 45 minutes.

UNIT                  WHAT IT IS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MLS-READ-01           Learning the First Eight Letter Sounds (4 weeks). Starts
                      fresh (no earlier unit needed).
MLS-READ-02           Blending Sounds into Words and Reading Sentences (2
                      weeks). Builds on MLS-READ-01.

Units that build on the same earlier unit, or start fresh, can run at the same
time.

The LSB framework functions purely as an adaptive, supportive scaffolding
framework for lesson content delivery. It does not prescribe operational
classroom routines, behavior management habits, or physical school rules.


==============================================================================
MLS-READ-01: Learning the First Eight Letter Sounds
4 weeks. Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
==============================================================================
Goal: Builds the eight letter sounds children need before they can blend and
read words in the next unit.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Show the first new letter on a card and say its sound
[changed: EB-04]      clearly. The whole class says the sound back three
                      times. Children trace the letter in the air, then on
                      their slates with a finger. Show the card again without
                      saying the sound while every child writes the letter on
                      their slate and holds it up, look at the slates, and say
                      the sound again if many are wrong before moving on.

2. Lead Educator      Children write the same letter with chalk five times on
                      their slates, copying any tone mark with the letter.
                      Check letter shapes as they write and correct formation
                      by showing the card again.

3. Lead Educator      Show two pictures of things whose names start with the
                      sound, chosen from familiar Thai animals, fruits, or
                      foods. The class says each name aloud and matches it to
                      the letter sound.

4. Lead Educator      Repeat the same routine for the next letters in textbook
                      order, two new letters each week, until all eight
                      letters are taught. Review known letters and sounds
                      briefly at the start of each lesson, after the wai
                      respect routine.

5. Lead Educator      In weeks 3 and 4, start two lessons with four known
[changed: EB-01]      letter sounds recalled from memory on slates with cards
                      put away. Children write the letters from the sounds you
                      say, then you show the cards so each child checks their
                      own. Nothing is collected, marked or recorded.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Letter cards, picture cards, slates and chalk are available.
  If not: Draw the letters and pictures with chalk on the board and have
  children trace and copy from the board; cover fewer new letters per lesson
  and combine practice steps in one period.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: every child answers
  together on slates held up, the educator looks at them, and re-says the
  sound if many are wrong before moving on
- Evidence-Based, EB-01 Retrieval Practice: short recall of known sounds from
  memory in weeks 3 and 4, self-checked against the cards


==============================================================================
MLS-READ-02: Blending Sounds into Words and Reading Sentences
2 weeks. Builds on MLS-READ-01.
==============================================================================
Goal: Turns the known letter sounds into blended word reading, daily word-list
practice, and each child reading a short sentence aloud.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Recall from MLS-READ-01: use the letter cards and sounds
                      children know. Write a short word made only from known
                      letters on the board, leaving wide spaces between words
                      at first since Thai normally runs together, starting
                      once at least three letters are known.

2. Lead Educator      Point to each letter while the class says its sound.
[changed: EB-04]      Sweep a finger under the whole word while the class
                      blends the sounds and says the word. After each word,
                      have every child write the word on their slate and hold
                      it up, look at all slates, and re-explain blending if
                      many are wrong before the next word. Do this with five
                      words each day.

3. Peer Facilitator   In pairs, children read the same five words to each
                      other. Each partner reads all five words while the other
                      listens, then they swap roles.

4. Lead Educator      End every lesson with ten minutes of reading practice
[changed: EB-06]      from the textbook word list. Keep all children in their
                      usual seats for the practice. First use one quick slate
                      round with a few words where each child shows current
                      reading to form two or three fluid groups by what they
                      show, then give each group words at its level for the
                      same ten minutes. Repeat the quick round as they
                      progress and move children between groups.

5. Lead Educator      At the end of the chapter, each child stands in front of
                      the class and reads aloud one short sentence made only
                      of learned words. Give each child one turn to read their
                      sentence.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Board, chalk and the textbook word list are available.
  If not: Copy the five daily words on reused chart paper before the lesson;
  if a week is lost, use three words per day and combine partner reading with
  the ten-minute practice in one period.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: every child shows blending
  on a slate each word, educator looks at all slates and re-explains if many
  are wrong
- Evidence-Based, EB-06 Teaching at the Right Level (level groups): practice
  starts from a fresh slate check into two or three fluid groups with words at
  each level for the same ten minutes, re-checked and regrouped
```
