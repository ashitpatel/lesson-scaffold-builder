```
==============================================================================
LESSON SCAFFOLD: Class 1 Language - Chapter 3: Letters, Sounds and First Words
==============================================================================
Goal: Children know the sounds of the first eight letters and read short words
made from them by blending.
Grade band: Primary. Period: 45 minutes.

UNIT                  WHAT IT IS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MLS-WORD-01           First Eight Letter Sounds (4 weeks). Starts fresh (no
                      earlier unit needed).
MLS-WORD-02           Blending Sounds into Words and Reading Sentences (2
                      weeks). Builds on MLS-WORD-01.

Units that build on the same earlier unit, or start fresh, can run at the same
time.

The LSB framework functions purely as an adaptive, supportive scaffolding
framework for lesson content delivery. It does not prescribe operational
classroom routines, behavior management habits, or physical school rules.


==============================================================================
MLS-WORD-01: First Eight Letter Sounds
4 weeks. Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
==============================================================================
Goal: Builds the eight letter-sound correspondences that later blending and
word reading depend on.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Explain that each letter stands for one sound and sounds
[changed: EB-04]      join to make words. Show each new letter on a card in
                      textbook order, two letters each week. Say each sound
                      clearly and have the whole class say it back three
                      times, then show a letter and have every child write it
                      on their slate and hold it up. Look at all the slates
                      and say the sound again if many are wrong before moving
                      on.

2. Lead Educator      Children trace each new letter in the air moving right
                      to left as school Arabic is written, then on their
                      slates with a finger. Then each child writes each letter
                      with chalk five times.

3. Lead Educator      Show two pictures of things whose names start with each
                      new sound using school Arabic names, not Darija home
                      words. Have the whole class say each name aloud.

4. Lead Educator      In weeks 2 to 4, open one lesson with four earlier
[changed: EB-01]      letters in their start, middle, and end word forms shown
                      one by one for children to say each sound from memory on
                      slates with cards put away in their box, kept clean and
                      off the floor. Then say each sound again so each child
                      checks their own. Nothing is collected or marked.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Letter cards, slates, chalk, and pictures for each new sound are
  available each lesson.
  If not: Draw the letter on the board and have children trace on desks and
  write with shared chalk. If a week is lost, teach three letters in one week
  by combining two picture routines in one period; do not extend past 4 weeks.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: every child answers on
  slates at the same time, educator looks at all answers and re-explains
  before moving on
- Evidence-Based, EB-01 Retrieval Practice: low-stakes recall of earlier
  letter sounds from memory in weeks 2 to 4, self-checked, nothing collected


==============================================================================
MLS-WORD-02: Blending Sounds into Words and Reading Sentences
2 weeks. Builds on MLS-WORD-01.
==============================================================================
Goal: Turns the known letter sounds into blended word reading, daily word-list
practice, and each child's own read-aloud sentence.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Recall from MLS-WORD-01: use only letters children
                      already know. Write one short word from those letters on
                      the board. Point to each letter while the class says its
                      sound, then sweep your finger under the word while the
                      class blends the sounds and says the word.

2. Lead Educator      Do five new words each day in the same way. Keep every
[changed: EB-04]      word made only from letters children have learned. After
                      each word, every child writes the word on a slate and
                      holds it up, you look at all slates and re-explain
                      blending if many are wrong before moving on.

3. Peer Facilitator   In pairs, children read the same five words to each
                      other. Each child reads all five words aloud while the
                      partner listens, then they swap.

4. Lead Educator      End each lesson with ten minutes of reading practice
[changed: EB-06]      from the textbook word list. Start practice with two
                      words every child reads on slates to form two fluid
                      groups by current reading, then each group reads
                      different words at its level for the same ten minutes.
                      Re-check and regroup as children progress. Nothing is
                      collected or marked.

5. Lead Educator      Listen to children read the week's words at the end of
                      each week. Record only the class total of words read on
                      chart paper, with no names or individual records.

6. Lead Educator      Have each child stand in front of the class and read
                      aloud one short sentence made of learned words. Give
                      each reader quiet time to finish.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Board, chalk, and the textbook word list are available for daily
  blending and reading practice.
  If not: Copy the five words onto chart paper for the day. If a week is lost,
  do three words per day instead of five and combine the weekly check with
  sentence reading in one period; do not extend past 2 weeks.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: every child answers on
  slates after each word, educator looks at all answers and re-explains before
  moving on
- Evidence-Based, EB-06 Teaching at the Right Level (level groups): ten-minute
  practice grouped by a fresh slate check into fluid groups reading different
  words at level for the same time, re-checked as they progress
```
