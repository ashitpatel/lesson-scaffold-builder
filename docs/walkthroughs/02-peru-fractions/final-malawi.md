==============================================================================
LESSON SCAFFOLD: Mathematics Grade 5 - Unit 4: Fractions in the Market and the
Garden
==============================================================================
Goal: Pupils represent, compare and add fractions and use them to describe how
a whole is shared.
Grade band: Primary. Period: 45 minutes.

UNIT                  WHAT IT IS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MLS-FRAC-01           Equal Parts and Equivalent Fractions by Folding (2
                      weeks). Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
MLS-FRAC-02           Comparing Fractions and Reading Market Quantities (2
                      weeks). Builds on MLS-FRAC-01.
MLS-FRAC-03           Sharing the Garden Bed and Making the Poster (2 weeks).
                      Builds on MLS-FRAC-01.

Units that build on the same earlier unit, or start fresh, can run at the same
time.

The LSB framework functions purely as an adaptive, supportive scaffolding
framework for lesson content delivery. It does not prescribe operational
classroom routines, behavior management habits, or physical school rules.


==============================================================================
MLS-FRAC-01: Equal Parts and Equivalent Fractions by Folding
2 weeks. Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
==============================================================================
Goal: Builds the equal-parts meaning of numerator and denominator and the
equivalent-fraction skill that comparing, adding and garden sharing all use.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Explain equal parts of a whole using a potato example
[changed: EB-04]      with one unequal cut. State that the bottom number names
                      the equal parts and the top number names the parts
                      taken. Every pupil writes on a slate whether two shown
                      cuts are equal parts and holds it up, then the educator
                      looks at all slates and re-explains if many are wrong
                      and moves on if most are right before pupils copy one
                      sentence and one example into notebooks.

2. Peer Facilitator   Give each pupil one paper strip to fold into halves,
[changed: CL-02]      then quarters, then eighths as for sharing nsima
                      portions equally. In mixed pairs, one pupil is the
                      Folder and the other is the Checker who checks folds are
                      equal before labelling, then they swap roles for the
                      next fold. The educator confirms any pair that is
                      unsure.

3. Lead Educator      Pupils lay the folded strips one above the other on
                      desks. Show that one half equals two quarters and four
                      eighths. Each pupil writes 1/2 = 2/4 = 4/8 in the
                      notebook.

4. Lead Educator      Give each pupil a second strip to fold into thirds and
[changed: EB-08]      then sixths. Work four board examples of equivalent
                      fractions by multiplying numerator and denominator by
                      the same number, pausing after each example for every
                      pupil to tell a neighbour why multiplying top and bottom
                      by the same number keeps the value. State the precise
                      reason straight after each pair-share before pupils copy
                      the four examples.

5. Lead Educator      Pupils complete twelve short textbook exercises on
                      equivalent fractions in notebooks. The educator marks
                      them during the lesson using the strips as reference.

6. Lead Educator      Count one class total of correct answers from the twelve
[changed: CL-04]      exercises on chart paper without names or scores. The
                      educator leads the count while pupils stay in the
                      classroom.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Paper strips, chart paper, chalk and notebooks are available.
  If not: Reuse scrap paper and combine steps 2 and 3 into one period; drop
  half of the twelve exercises.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: every pupil answers on
  slates during the equal-parts explanation and the educator re-explains or
  moves on based on all answers
- Evidence-Based, EB-08 Self-Explanation: after each equivalent-fraction
  example every pupil explains why the step works to a neighbour and the
  educator states the precise reason straight after
- Connected Learning, CL-02 Structural Peer Roles: fold checking done in mixed
  pairs with rotating Folder and Checker roles
- Connected Learning, CL-04 Collective Shared Purpose: one class total of
  correct answers on chart paper without names or scores


==============================================================================
MLS-FRAC-02: Comparing Fractions and Reading Market Quantities
2 weeks. Builds on MLS-FRAC-01.
==============================================================================
Goal: Builds accurate comparing of fractions with the same denominator and the
same numerator, applied to quarter-kilo and half-kilo quantities as fractions
of one kilo.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Recall from MLS-FRAC-01: use the folded strips showing
                      1/2 = 2/4 = 4/8. Compare 3/8 and 5/8 with the same
                      denominator using the strips. Each pupil writes which is
                      larger and why in one sentence.

2. Lead Educator      Compare 2/3 and 2/5 with the same numerator like shared
[changed: EB-04]      harvest portions on weekly market day using the thirds
                      and eighths strips. Each pupil writes the order on a
                      slate or scrap paper and holds it up at the same time.
                      The educator looks at all the answers and re-explains
                      the comparison if many are wrong before moving on.

3. Lead Educator      Show two pre-weighed piles of dry maize kernels
                      (chimanga) labelled one quarter kilo and one half kilo.
                      Each pupil names each pile as a fraction of one kilo and
                      says which is larger. No money is used and nothing is
                      bought or sold.

4. Lead Educator      Pupils complete short notebook exercises comparing pairs
[changed: EB-07]      of fractions with the same denominator and the same
                      numerator, mixed across both types in shuffled order,
                      keeping the same items and total count. Before each
                      pair, each pupil writes which rule fits the pair before
                      solving it. The educator marks them in class against the
                      strips. Each pupil corrects errors in the same lesson.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Dry beans or stones for two demonstration piles are available.
  If not: Use only the folded strips for quarter and half comparisons and
  combine steps 3 and 4 into one period.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: every pupil answers the
  same-numerator comparison at the same time on slates or scrap, educator
  looks at all answers and re-explains if many are wrong
- Evidence-Based, EB-07 Interleaved Practice: same comparing items shuffled
  across both rules with a which-rule-fits prompt answered before each pair


==============================================================================
MLS-FRAC-03: Sharing the Garden Bed and Making the Poster
2 weeks. Builds on MLS-FRAC-01.
==============================================================================
Goal: Turns representing, equivalent forms and same-denominator adding into
the shared-bed description and each pupil's finished poster of the shares.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Recall from MLS-FRAC-01: use the folded strips and the
                      rule for equivalent fractions. Review numerators,
                      denominators and one example of equal parts. Each pupil
                      writes one equivalent pair in the notebook.

2. Lead Educator      The whole class goes to the garden bed together to see
                      the teacher's example ridges with the educator staying
                      with all groups. Look at the rectangular bed marked as
                      eight one-metre squares. Pupils must not pick leaves,
                      touch tools, or lift heavy objects.

3. Lead Educator      Announce the rule first: each pupil gets one
[changed: PB-04]      show-of-hands vote for where the two spare squares go,
                      most votes wins. Draw the bed on the board as eight
                      squares with potatoes at least two squares, beans at
                      least one and maize at least three. Hold the vote, write
                      the chosen shares, the rule and what is planted next on
                      chart paper, then each pupil copies the grid and writes
                      each crop share as a fraction of the whole bed.

4. Lead Educator      Explain adding fractions with the same denominator by
[changed: EB-04]      adding numerators and keeping the denominator. Add one
                      garden-share sum of teacher's example harvest portions
                      on the board step by step. Ask one same-denominator
                      question that every pupil answers at the same time on
                      scrap paper held up, look at all the answers, and
                      re-explain before moving on if many are wrong. Each
                      pupil copies the sum in the notebook.

5. Lead Educator      Give a full lesson of quiet work time for the poster
[changed: CL-05]      draft. Each pupil draws the shared bed called munda in
                      Chichewa, writes each crop share as a fraction in two
                      equivalent ways, and adds two shares. Write it so a
                      younger pupil can follow it. The educator circulates
                      without collecting extra paperwork.

6. Peer Facilitator   In pairs, read each other's draft and check it against
                      the garden grid and the two equivalent forms. Each
                      partner writes one note on what is missing or unclear,
                      then partners swap roles.

7. Lead Educator      Give a second full lesson for each pupil to correct the
                      draft using the partner note and write the final copy.
                      Each pupil keeps their name on their own work only.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: The garden bed is accessible in dry weather for one short visit.
  If not: Skip the visit and use only the board grid; shorten step 5 drafting
  and drop one equivalent form.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: every pupil answers one
  same-denominator question on held-up scrap paper during the explanation and
  the educator re-explains if many are wrong
- Connected Learning, CL-05 Real Audience Production: poster draft written so
  a younger pupil can follow it as the real audience
- Participatory Budgeting, PB-04 Shared Resource Round: educator-only
  assignment replaced by announced one-vote show-of-hands round for the two
  spare squares, with result, rule and next planting written on chart paper
  (PB-05 folded in)
