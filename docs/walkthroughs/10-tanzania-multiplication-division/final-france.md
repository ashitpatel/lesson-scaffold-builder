==============================================================================
LESSON SCAFFOLD: Grade 4 Mathematics - Multiplication and Division Practice
==============================================================================
Goal: Pupils practise multiplying two-digit numbers and dividing with
remainders, including word problems and mixed sets.
Grade band: Primary. Period: 45 minutes.

UNIT                  WHAT IT IS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MLS-MULDIV-01         Multiplying Two-Digit Numbers (3 weeks). Starts fresh
                      (no earlier unit needed).
MLS-MULDIV-02         Division With Remainders and Sharing Problems (3 weeks).
                      Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
MLS-MULDIV-03         Mixed Multiplication and Division Set (2 weeks). Builds
                      on MLS-MULDIV-01, MLS-MULDIV-02.

Units that build on the same earlier unit, or start fresh, can run at the same
time.

The LSB framework functions purely as an adaptive, supportive scaffolding
framework for lesson content delivery. It does not prescribe operational
classroom routines, behavior management habits, or physical school rules.


==============================================================================
MLS-MULDIV-01: Multiplying Two-Digit Numbers
3 weeks. Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
==============================================================================
Goal: Builds accurate two-digit by one-digit multiplication that later mixed
sets draw on.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Show 23 x 4 on the board and explain tens and ones in
[changed: EB-04]      two short sentences. Pupils copy the worked example into
                      their notebooks. Ask two questions every pupil answers
                      at once on slates held up during the daily calcul mental
                      ritual, look at all slates, and re-explain the tens step
                      if many are wrong before moving on. Nothing is collected
                      or marked.

2. Lead Educator      At the start of practice, ask three short multiplication
[changed: EB-06]      items every pupil answers on slates held up. Use only
                      this check to form two or three fluid groups for the
                      lesson using colour progress bands, with nothing marked
                      or displayed.

3. Lead Educator      Give a full lesson of quiet solving time. Each group
[changed: EB-06,      solves twenty multiplication problems alone in a
CL-03]                notebook at its level for the same minutes, such as 21
                      benches x 2 and 31 sticks x 2 for one group and 42
                      windows x 3 and 53 sticks x 2 for another. Re-check with
                      two slate items halfway and move pupils between groups
                      as they progress.

4. Lead Educator      Add the class total of correct answers on chart paper
                      with chalk. Record only the class total, not individual
                      scores or names.

5. Peer Facilitator   In pairs, pupils take turns asking multiplication tables
                      and checking answers in their notebooks. Swap roles
                      halfway so each pupil asks and answers.

6. Peer Facilitator   Exchange notebooks with another pair and mark answers
                      against the board answers. Return each notebook to its
                      owner with no scores copied elsewhere.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Board, chalk, pupil notebooks and one chart paper sheet available.
  If not: If chart paper is missing, keep totals on the board. If one week is
  lost, combine steps 4 and 5 into one period and drop the second tables
  round.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: every pupil answers two
  questions on slates during the explanation, educator looks and re-explains
  if many are wrong
- Evidence-Based, EB-06 Teaching at the Right Level (level groups): quick
  fresh slate check forming two or three fluid unmarked groups
- Evidence-Based, EB-06 Teaching at the Right Level (level groups): same
  lesson time on same multiplication skill at group level with halfway
  re-check and movement
- Connected Learning, CL-03 Real-World Context Mapping: same twenty
  multiplication problems framed with everyday school counts


==============================================================================
MLS-MULDIV-02: Division With Remainders and Sharing Problems
3 weeks. Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
==============================================================================
Goal: Builds division with remainders and equal-sharing word problems that
later mixed sets draw on.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Explain remainders step by step on the board using 17
[changed: EB-03]      divided by 3 with sticks or bottles from the classroom
                      bûchettes box to show the leftover, stating how each
                      stick matches the numbers. Show 19 divided by 4 with its
                      last step blank for pupils to finish in notebooks, then
                      show the answer.

2. Lead Educator      Give a full lesson of quiet solving time. Each pupil
                      solves fifteen division problems alone in a notebook,
                      such as 17 divided by 3, 23 divided by 5 and 31 divided
                      by 4.

3. Lead Educator      Read aloud ten word problems with French first names
                      like Léa and Hugo about sharing mangoes equally among
                      baskets. Each pupil solves them alone in a notebook and
                      writes the remainder where needed.

4. Peer Facilitator   In pairs, take turns as Reader and Checker to compare
[changed: CL-02]      answers against the board answers and point to any
                      missed remainder, swapping roles halfway. Return each
                      notebook to its owner with no scores copied elsewhere.

5. Lead Educator      Add the class total of correct answers on chart paper
                      with chalk. Record only the class total, not individual
                      scores or names.

6. Lead Educator      In week 3, open one lesson with four division questions
[changed: EB-01]      from memory on slates, with notebooks and charts out of
                      sight. Show the answers straight after so each pupil
                      checks their own. Nothing is collected or marked.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Board, chalk, pupil notebooks, sticks or empty bottles for showing
  remainders.
  If not: If counters are missing, draw groups on the board. If one week is
  lost, solve six sharing problems instead of ten and combine steps 4 and 5
  into one period.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-03 Worked Examples, then Practice: complete step-by-step
  example plus a second example with its last step left for pupils to finish
  before independent practice
- Evidence-Based, EB-01 Retrieval Practice: low-stakes recall of division from
  memory in week 3, self-checked with nothing collected
- Connected Learning, CL-02 Structural Peer Roles: same pair answer-check done
  with rotating Reader and Checker roles, swapping halfway


==============================================================================
MLS-MULDIV-03: Mixed Multiplication and Division Set
2 weeks. Builds on MLS-MULDIV-01, MLS-MULDIV-02.
==============================================================================
Goal: Combines the multiplication and division skills into one mixed worksheet
so pupils practise both operations taught in earlier weeks.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Recall from MLS-MULDIV-01 and MLS-MULDIV-02: use the
                      class charts for multiplying two-digit numbers and
                      dividing with remainders. Each pupil copies a mixed
                      worksheet of ten multiplication and ten division
                      problems including small euro amounts into a notebook.

2. Lead Educator      Give a full lesson of quiet solving time. Each pupil
[changed: EB-07]      solves the same twenty problems in shuffled order,
                      mixing multiplication and division, in a notebook.
                      Before each problem, each pupil writes which operation
                      fits, then solves.

3. Peer Facilitator   In mixed pairs, check answers against the board answers
[changed: CL-02]      for both operations, with one partner as Checker and the
                      other as Recorder, then swap roles. Return each notebook
                      to its owner with no scores copied elsewhere.

4. Lead Educator      Add the class total of correct answers on chart paper
                      with chalk. Keep the chart for the next mixed set.

5. Lead Educator      In week 2, open one lesson with four short problems from
[changed: EB-01]      memory on scrap paper as a low-stakes personal-best
                      race, with charts and notebooks put away. Show the
                      answers straight after so each pupil checks their own.
                      Nothing is collected or marked.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Board, chalk, pupil notebooks and prior class charts available.
  If not: If charts are missing, rewrite the two worked examples on the board.
  If one week is lost, use ten problems instead of twenty and check them in
  the same period.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-07 Interleaved Practice: same twenty problems shuffled to
  mix operations, with a choose-the-operation line answered before each solve
- Evidence-Based, EB-01 Retrieval Practice: low-stakes week-2 recall of mixed
  problems from memory, self-checked, nothing collected
- Connected Learning, CL-02 Structural Peer Roles: pair checking uses rotating
  Checker and Recorder roles with a swap
