==============================================================================
LESSON SCAFFOLD: Grade 4 Mathematics - Multiplication and Division Practice
==============================================================================
Goal: Pupils multiply two-digit numbers and divide with remainders, including
in market and sharing word problems.
Grade band: Primary. Period: 45 minutes.

UNIT                  WHAT IT IS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MLS-MULTDIV-01        Multiplying Two-Digit Numbers (3 weeks). Starts fresh
                      (no earlier unit needed).
MLS-MULTDIV-02        Division With Remainders and Sharing Problems (3 weeks).
                      Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
MLS-MULTDIV-03        Mixed Multiplication and Division Set (2 weeks). Builds
                      on MLS-MULTDIV-01, MLS-MULTDIV-02.

Units that build on the same earlier unit, or start fresh, can run at the same
time.

The LSB framework functions purely as an adaptive, supportive scaffolding
framework for lesson content delivery. It does not prescribe operational
classroom routines, behavior management habits, or physical school rules.


==============================================================================
MLS-MULTDIV-01: Multiplying Two-Digit Numbers
3 weeks. Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
==============================================================================
Goal: Builds accurate two-digit multiplication and teacher-provided market
word problems for the mixed work in the final unit.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Show 23 x 4 on the board and explain each multiplying
[changed: EB-04]      step. Pupils copy the example into their notebooks. Then
                      give two short problems for every pupil to solve on
                      slates at once and hold up, look at all slates, and
                      re-explain the steps if many are wrong before moving on.

2. Lead Educator      In week 1, give three short multiplication problems for
[changed: EB-06]      every pupil to solve on slates at once and hold up.
                      Using what they show today, form two or three fluid
                      groups for practice and re-check with slates as they
                      progress. Nothing is marked or recorded.

3. Lead Educator      Give groups a paper mock ledger on the board: a made-up
[changed: EB-06,      budget of 400 points and six options, each with a
CL-01, PB-01]         made-up two-digit quantity and single-digit per-item
                      points. Groups multiply to work out each option cost,
                      then choose options whose total fits under 400 points,
                      writing the sum, and say what they gave up. Each pupil
                      solves their group's chosen set alone in their notebooks
                      using the same answer chart.

4. Peer Facilitator   In pairs with rotating questioner roles, pupils practise
                      multiplication tables aloud. The questioner checks
                      answers against the notebook table.

5. Lead Educator      Write two market amounts on chart paper. Each pupil
                      writes three multiplication word problems using those
                      amounts and solves them.

6. Peer Facilitator   In pairs, pupils exchange notebooks and check work
                      against the answer chart on the board, then return
                      books. No scores are recorded or announced.

7. Lead Educator      Collect one solved word problem from each group onto one
[changed: CL-04]      class chart on chart paper to make a combined class set,
                      without names or scores. Read the combined set aloud
                      together.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Chalk, board, notebooks and reused chart paper are available.
  If not: Copy the example and amounts on the board only and have pupils work
  in notebooks; combine steps 2 and 4 in one period.
- Needs: A full 3-week run with no lost week.
  If not: Drop one practice round from step 2 and shorten pair practice to one
  session; do not extend the unit.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: every pupil answers two
  short problems on slates at once, educator looks at all and re-explains if
  many are wrong
- Evidence-Based, EB-06 Teaching at the Right Level (level groups): fresh
  slate level check forming two or three fluid groups, re-checked as they
  progress, nothing marked
- Evidence-Based, EB-06 Teaching at the Right Level (level groups): same
  twenty-problem practice in level sets with the same minutes on the same
  skill per group
- Connected Learning, CL-01 Interest Integration: same practice offered as
  sports scores or crop counts with same numbers and answer chart
- Connected Learning, CL-04 Collective Shared Purpose: one solved problem per
  group combined into one class set without names or scores
- Participatory Budgeting, PB-01 Knapsack Ledger: generic group sets replaced
  by made-up 400-point ledger with six costed options requiring two-digit
  multiplication


==============================================================================
MLS-MULTDIV-02: Division With Remainders and Sharing Problems
3 weeks. Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
==============================================================================
Goal: Builds division with remainders and equal-sharing word solutions that
the final mixed set draws on.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Show 17 divided by 3 on the board and explain each
[changed: EB-03]      dividing step, showing what is left over. Show 19
                      divided by 4 with its last step left blank for pupils to
                      finish in their notebooks, then show the answer. Pupils
                      copy both examples into their notebooks.

2. Lead Educator      Give fifteen division problems such as 17 divided by 3,
[changed: CL-01]      23 divided by 5 and 31 divided by 4 in two themes with
                      the same divisors, mango baskets and market counts. Each
                      pupil chooses one theme and solves its set alone in
                      their notebooks.

3. Peer Facilitator   In pairs with rotating questioner roles, pupils practise
                      division facts aloud. The questioner checks answers
                      against the notebook.

4. Lead Educator      Read ten word problems about sharing mangoes equally
                      among baskets. Pupils solve them alone in their
                      notebooks.

5. Peer Facilitator   In pairs, pupils exchange notebooks and check division
                      work against the answer chart, then return books. No
                      scores are recorded or announced.

6. Lead Educator      In week 3, open one lesson with four division questions
[changed: EB-01]      answered from memory on scrap paper, with notebooks and
                      charts put away. Show the answers straight after so each
                      pupil checks their own. Nothing is collected or marked.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Chalk, board, notebooks and reused chart paper are available.
  If not: Use the board answer chart only and have pupils solve fewer problems
  per session; combine steps 2 and 4.
- Needs: A full 3-week run with no lost week.
  If not: Use five mango problems instead of ten and drop the second pair
  session; do not extend the unit.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-03 Worked Examples, then Practice: one complete remainder
  example shown step by step, then a second example left for pupils to finish
  before independent practice
- Evidence-Based, EB-01 Retrieval Practice: low-stakes recall of division from
  memory in week 3, self-checked, nothing collected
- Connected Learning, CL-01 Interest Integration: same fifteen divisions
  offered in two everyday themes with pupil choice of theme


==============================================================================
MLS-MULTDIV-03: Mixed Multiplication and Division Set
2 weeks. Builds on MLS-MULTDIV-01, MLS-MULTDIV-02.
==============================================================================
Goal: Combines the earlier multiplication and division skills into one
Friday-style mixed problem set completed in class.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Recall from MLS-MULTDIV-01 and MLS-MULTDIV-02: use the
                      class chart of multiplication and division methods.

2. Lead Educator      Give ten multiplication and division problems in two
[changed: CL-03]      labelled blocks on the board, multiplication first and
                      division second, using everyday quantities and a made-up
                      price list of everyday goods. Pupils solve them alone in
                      their notebooks.

3. Lead Educator      Give another ten multiplication and division problems in
                      two labelled blocks on the board, multiplication first
                      and division second. Pupils solve them alone in their
                      notebooks.

4. Peer Facilitator   In pairs with rotating roles, pupils compare answers
                      against the board chart. Each partner points to one step
                      to fix.

5. Lead Educator      Give class time for pupils who have not finished to
                      complete the same board problems. No extra books are
                      needed and no answers are copied.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Chalk, board and notebooks for the 20-problem mixed set.
  If not: Run ten problems total, five per block, on the board; combine steps
  2 and 3 in one period.
- Needs: A full 2-week run with no lost week.
  If not: Drop step 5 rework time and finish checking in pairs; do not extend
  the unit.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Connected Learning, CL-03 Real-World Context Mapping: same ten mixed
  problems using everyday quantities and a made-up everyday price list
