==============================================================================
LESSON SCAFFOLD: Grade 4 Mathematics - Multiplication and Division Practice
==============================================================================
Goal: Pupils practise multiplying two-digit numbers and dividing with
remainders, including word problems and Friday mixed sets.
Grade band: Primary. Period: 45 minutes.

UNIT                  WHAT IT IS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MLS-MULDIV-01         Multiplying Two-Digit Numbers (3 weeks). Starts fresh
                      (no earlier unit needed).
MLS-MULDIV-02         Division With Remainders and Sharing Problems (3 weeks).
                      Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
MLS-MULDIV-03         Friday Mixed Multiplication and Division Sets (2 weeks).
                      Builds on MLS-MULDIV-01, MLS-MULDIV-02.

Units that build on the same earlier unit, or start fresh, can run at the same
time.

The LSB framework functions purely as an adaptive, supportive scaffolding
framework for lesson content delivery. It does not prescribe operational
classroom routines, behavior management habits, or physical school rules.


==============================================================================
MLS-MULDIV-01: Multiplying Two-Digit Numbers
3 weeks. Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
==============================================================================
Goal: Builds fluent two-digit multiplication and table recall that the later
mixed sets draw on.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Show one example on the chalkboard, 23 times 4, and
[changed: EB-03]      explain each place-value step in short sentences. Show a
                      second example with its last step left blank for pupils
                      to finish in their notebooks, then show the answer.
                      Pupils copy both examples into their notebooks.

2. Lead Educator      Give pupils twenty similar problems to solve alone in
[changed: CL-01]      their notebooks, starting with 31 times 2, 42 times 3
                      and 53 times 2. Each pupil chooses one of two themes for
                      the same problems, sports scores or crop growth,like
                      taekwondo points or napa cabbage (baechu) growth in
                      class with the same numbers and the same answer check.
                      Allow quiet work time for the full period.

3. Lead Educator      Put pupils in mixed pairs that change weekly. Pairs take
                      turns asking table questions and answering, swapping
                      roles halfway through.

4. Peer Facilitator   In mixed pairs, pupils take turns asking table questions
                      and checking answers, swapping roles halfway through.

5. Peer Facilitator   In mixed pairs, pupils exchange notebooks and mark each
                      other's multiplication answers. The educator collects
                      the books.

6. Lead Educator      In week 3, open two lessons with four multiplication
[changed: EB-01]      questions answered from memory on scrap paper, with
                      notebooks closed and put away. Then show the answers so
                      each pupil checks their own. Nothing is collected,
                      marked or recorded.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Chalkboard and pupil notebooks available each period.
  If not: Combine the two solo problem sets into one period and drop one
  tables round that week to stay in 3 weeks.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-03 Worked Examples, then Practice: second worked example
  left for pupils to finish before independent practice
- Evidence-Based, EB-01 Retrieval Practice: low-stakes recall of
  multiplication from memory in week 3, self-checked
- Connected Learning, CL-01 Interest Integration: same twenty multiplications
  with a choice of two themes, sports scores or crop growth, and the same
  answer check


==============================================================================
MLS-MULDIV-02: Division With Remainders and Sharing Problems
3 weeks. Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
==============================================================================
Goal: Builds division with remainders and equal-sharing word answers that the
later mixed sets draw on.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Show one complete division example step by step on the
[changed: EB-03]      chalkboard, showing what is left over after equal
                      sharing. Show a second example with its last step left
                      blank for pupils to finish on slates, then show the
                      answer. Pupils copy both examples into their notebooks.

2. Lead Educator      Give pupils fifteen division problems to solve alone in
[changed: CL-01]      notebooks, starting with 17 divided by 3, 23 divided by
                      5 and 31 divided by 4. Each pupil chooses whether the
                      numbers stand for tangerines (gyul) shared among baskets
                      or stones shared among groups, using the same numbers.
                      Allow quiet work time for the full period.

3. Lead Educator      Show sharing tangerines (gyul) equally among baskets on
[changed: EB-04]      the chalkboard, using drawn baskets and small stones for
                      tangerines (gyul). Show where the remainder sits after
                      sharing. Ask one sharing question with every pupil
                      holding up fingers for the remainder at the same time,
                      look at all answers, and re-explain the leftover if many
                      are wrong before moving on.

4. Lead Educator      Give pupils ten word problems about sharing tangerines
                      (gyul) equally among baskets to solve alone in
                      notebooks. Each answer gives baskets filled and
                      tangerines (gyul) left over.

5. Peer Facilitator   In mixed pairs, pupils exchange notebooks and mark each
                      other's division answers.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Chalkboard, notebooks and small stones for sharing demo available.
  If not: Draw baskets and tangerines (gyul) tallies on the chalkboard with no
  stones, and combine the demo with the first word problems in one period.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-03 Worked Examples, then Practice: one complete example
  is shown step by step, then a second example is left for pupils to finish
  before the independent problems
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: every pupil answers one
  remainder question at the same time on fingers while the educator looks and
  re-explains if many are wrong
- Connected Learning, CL-01 Interest Integration: same fifteen problems with
  pupil choice of fruit-baskets or stones-groups framing


==============================================================================
MLS-MULDIV-03: Friday Mixed Multiplication and Division Sets
2 weeks. Builds on MLS-MULDIV-01, MLS-MULDIV-02.
==============================================================================
Goal: Combines the earlier multiplication skill and remainder skill into
Friday mixed blocks of both operations.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Recall from MLS-MULDIV-01 and MLS-MULDIV-02: use the
                      solved two-digit multiplications and the remainder rule
                      from equal sharing. Pupils solve one worksheet alone
                      using only teacher-given figures set at a traditional
                      market or a convenience mart with ten multiplication
                      problems first and ten division problems second.

2. Lead Educator      Give a second Friday worksheet alone with ten new
                      multiplication problems first and ten new division
                      problems second. Pupils work straight through in order
                      in their notebooks.All practice stays in class with no
                      extra homework.

3. Peer Facilitator   In mixed pairs, pupils exchange notebooks and mark each
[changed: CL-02]      other's mixed answers. One pupil reads each answer aloud
                      while the other checks it, then they swap Reader and
                      Checker roles for the second half.

4. Lead Educator      Give pupils ten similar mixed problems to solve alone in
[changed: EB-07]      notebooks for fluency, with multiplication and division
                      in mixed order. Pupils say which method fits each item
                      before solving it.

5. Lead Educator      On the chalkboard make one class total of correct
[changed: CL-04]      answers, without names or individual scores. Each pair
                      adds its count to the running total so only the combined
                      class total remains visible.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Mixed worksheets available, or chalkboard space to copy the set.
  If not: Copy the ten-plus-ten set on the chalkboard and drop the final
  fluency set to stay in 2 weeks.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-07 Interleaved Practice: the final ten-item set is
  shuffled across both operations with a which-method prompt before each item,
  after blocked practice in steps 1 and 2
- Connected Learning, CL-02 Structural Peer Roles: named Reader and Checker
  roles with swap for existing peer marking
- Connected Learning, CL-04 Collective Shared Purpose: class total of correct
  answers built from pairs without names or individual scores
