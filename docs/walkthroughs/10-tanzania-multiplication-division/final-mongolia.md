==============================================================================
LESSON SCAFFOLD: Grade 4 Mathematics - Multiplication and Division Practice
==============================================================================
Goal: Pupils practise multiplying two-digit numbers and dividing with
remainders, including market word problems and weekly mixed sets.
Grade band: Primary. Period: 45 minutes.

UNIT                  WHAT IT IS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MLS-MULDIV-01         Multiplying Two-Digit Numbers (2 weeks). Starts fresh
                      (no earlier unit needed).
MLS-MULDIV-02         Division With Remainders (2 weeks). Starts fresh (no
                      earlier unit needed).
MLS-MULDIV-03         Market Word Problems (3 weeks). Builds on MLS-MULDIV-01,
                      MLS-MULDIV-02.
MLS-MULDIV-04         Mixed Practice and Weekly Set (3 weeks). Builds on
                      MLS-MULDIV-01, MLS-MULDIV-02.

Units that build on the same earlier unit, or start fresh, can run at the same
time.

The LSB framework functions purely as an adaptive, supportive scaffolding
framework for lesson content delivery. It does not prescribe operational
classroom routines, behavior management habits, or physical school rules.


==============================================================================
MLS-MULDIV-01: Multiplying Two-Digit Numbers
2 weeks. Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
==============================================================================
Goal: Builds fluent two-digit by one-digit multiplication that later word
problems and mixed sets use.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Show 23 × 4 on the board in place-value steps and
[changed: EB-04]      explain each step once. Pupils copy the worked example
                      into their notebooks for reference. Then each pupil
                      answers two short questions on slates inside the warm
                      classroom and holds them up at the same time. Look at
                      all slates and re-explain any step many missed before
                      moving on.

2. Lead Educator      Give ten similar two-digit by one-digit problems on the
                      board, such as 31 × 2 and 42 × 3. Each pupil solves all
                      ten alone in their notebook using the board method.

3. Lead Educator      Give a second set of ten similar problems on the board,
                      such as 53 × 2. Each pupil solves all ten alone in their
                      notebook with quiet writing time for the full lesson.

4. Peer Facilitator   In pairs, exchange notebooks and mark the twenty answers
[changed: CL-02]      against the board answers. One partner takes the Reader
                      role and reads each board answer aloud while the other
                      takes the Checker role and marks it, then they swap
                      roles after ten answers. Each pupil corrects their own
                      errors in their notebook.

5. Lead Educator      In week 2, open one lesson with four short
[changed: EB-01]      multiplication questions from week 1 answered from
                      memory on scrap paper, with notebooks and books put
                      away. Then show the answers on the board so each pupil
                      checks their own. Nothing is collected or marked.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Chalk, board, notebooks and chart paper for the multiplication chart
  are available.
  If not: Combine the two ten-problem sets into one lesson and drop the second
  peer-check round; do not extend past 2 weeks.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: every pupil answers two
  short questions on slates at the same time and the educator re-explains
  missed steps before practice
- Evidence-Based, EB-01 Retrieval Practice: week-2 low-stakes recall of the
  method from memory with answers shown for self-checking
- Connected Learning, CL-02 Structural Peer Roles: pairs mark with rotating
  Reader and Checker roles, swapping after ten answers


==============================================================================
MLS-MULDIV-02: Division With Remainders
2 weeks. Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
==============================================================================
Goal: Builds division-with-remainder skill that later word problems and mixed
sets combine with multiplication.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Show 17 ÷ 3 on the board in sharing steps and explain
[changed: EB-03]      each step aloud, then show 19 ÷ 4 fully. Write 23 ÷ 5
                      with its last step left blank for pupils to finish in
                      their notebooks, then show the answer.

2. Lead Educator      Give eight division problems with remainders on the
[changed: CL-03]      board using everyday sharing counts seen at school,like
                      sheep, goats, and bowls of milk, such as 23 ÷ 5 as
                      sharing sticks among groups. Each pupil solves all eight
                      alone in their notebook.

3. Lead Educator      Give seven more division problems with remainders, such
                      as 31 ÷ 4. Each pupil solves all seven alone with quiet
                      writing time for the full lesson.

4. Peer Facilitator   In pairs, exchange notebooks and mark the fifteen
                      answers against the board answers. Each pupil corrects
                      their own errors in their notebook.

5. Lead Educator      In week 2, open two lessons with three
[changed: EB-01]      division-with-remainder questions answered from memory
                      on scrap paper, with notebooks and books out of sight.
                      Then show the answers so each pupil checks their own.
                      Nothing is collected or marked.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Chalk, board, notebooks and chart paper for the remainder chart are
  available.
  If not: Combine the eight- and seven-problem sets into one lesson with fewer
  items and drop one correction round; do not extend past 2 weeks.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-03 Worked Examples, then Practice: complete examples
  shown step by step with the last example left for pupils to finish before
  independent practice
- Evidence-Based, EB-01 Retrieval Practice: low-stakes recall of the remainder
  method from memory in week 2, self-checked
- Connected Learning, CL-03 Real-World Context Mapping: grounded the eight
  abstract problems in school-observable sharing counts


==============================================================================
MLS-MULDIV-03: Market Word Problems
3 weeks. Builds on MLS-MULDIV-01, MLS-MULDIV-02.
==============================================================================
Goal: Applies multiplication and division to market and mango-sharing word
problems where pupils write and solve their own items.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Recall from MLS-MULDIV-01: two-digit by one-digit
                      products such as 31 x 2, and from MLS-MULDIV-02:
                      remainders such as 19 ÷ 4, using the board method and
                      notebooks. Each pupil writes three multiplication word
                      problems about soum market using board amounts.

2. Lead Educator      Solve one division word problem about sharing mangoes
[changed: EB-03,      equally among baskets step by step on the board, saying
PB-01]                each step aloud.

3. Peer Facilitator   In pairs, use the mock ledger on the board: a made-up
[changed: PB-01]      budget of 500 togrog and six pre-written made-up costs
                      for market options. Pairs choose options whose total
                      fits under 500 togrog, writing the sums, and say what
                      they gave up. Partners take turns: one works, the other
                      checks, then they swap.

4. Lead Educator      Give a full lesson of quiet writing time. Each pupil
                      completes any unfinished word problems and writes full
                      number sentences for each answer.

5. Peer Facilitator   In pairs, exchange notebooks and check answers against
                      the board answers. Each pupil corrects their own errors
                      in their notebook.

6. Lead Educator      In week 3, open one lesson with four short
[changed: EB-01]      multiplication and division word questions answered from
                      memory on scrap paper, with charts and notebooks put
                      away. Show the answers on the board straight after so
                      each pupil checks their own. Nothing is collected or
                      marked.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Board, notebooks and chart paper for word-problem amounts are
  available; all work stays in class.
  If not: If a week is lost, drop the quiet-writing lesson and finish
  remaining items as one shorter set in class; do not extend past 3 weeks.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-03 Worked Examples, then Practice: one complete division
  word problem modelled step by step plus a second left for pupils to finish
  before the ten solo problems
- Evidence-Based, EB-01 Retrieval Practice: low-stakes recall of
  multiplication and division word content from memory in week 3, self-checked
- Participatory Budgeting, PB-01 Knapsack Ledger: reduced bundled division
  block to single modelled mango-sharing example on the board
- Participatory Budgeting, PB-01 Knapsack Ledger: added pairs mock ledger with
  500-point budget and six pre-written made-up costs; pairs choose a fitting
  set, write sums and state what was given up


==============================================================================
MLS-MULDIV-04: Mixed Practice and Weekly Set
3 weeks. Builds on MLS-MULDIV-01, MLS-MULDIV-02.
==============================================================================
Goal: Consolidates both operations in extra practice, a blocked weekly mixed
set, and pair table practice with peer checking.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Recall from MLS-MULDIV-01: two-digit products such as 23
                      x 4, and from MLS-MULDIV-02: sharing-with-remainder
                      results such as 17 ÷ 3, using the board method and
                      notebooks. Each pupil solves six extra practice problems
                      from the board alone in their notebook.

2. Lead Educator      Give one worksheet on chart paper with twenty
[changed: CL-03]      multiplication and division problems in two labelled
                      blocks, ten multiplication first then ten division,
                      using counts seen on the school grounds such as desks,
                      sticks and stones or sheep, goats, and kindling counted
                      indoors. Each pupil solves all twenty alone in their
                      notebook.

3. Peer Facilitator   In mixed pairs that re-form each session,mixing younger
                      and older pupils across grades, pupils practise
                      multiplication table questions using notebooks and the
                      board table. Roles alternate each session so each pupil
                      takes turns asking and checking answers and answering.

4. Peer Facilitator   In pairs, exchange notebooks and mark the mixed set
                      against the board answers. Each pupil corrects their own
                      errors in their notebook.

5. Lead Educator      Make one class total on chart paper of correct answers
[changed: CL-04]      from the weekly set,counted by Thursday in the boarding
                      week, with no names or pair scores shown or kept. Each
                      pair counts its own correct answers quietly and tells
                      the educator the number, and the educator adds them into
                      a single total.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Chart paper for the weekly mixed set and board space for extra
  problems are available.
  If not: If a week is lost, use three class sessions instead of four by
  halving the mixed set to five plus five and combining pair practice with
  marking; do not extend past 3 weeks.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Connected Learning, CL-03 Real-World Context Mapping: blocked set uses
  counts seen on the school grounds
- Connected Learning, CL-04 Collective Shared Purpose: single anonymous class
  total of correct answers from the weekly set
