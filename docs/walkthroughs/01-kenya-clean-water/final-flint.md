```
==============================================================================
LESSON SCAFFOLD: Grade 6 Science and Technology - Strand 3: Clean Water and
Health
==============================================================================
Goal: Learners explain how water becomes unsafe and describe and demonstrate
safe ways to treat and store drinking water.
Grade band: Primary. Period: 45 minutes.

UNIT                  WHAT IT IS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MLS-WATER-01          How Water Becomes Unsafe and Safe Storage (2 weeks).
                      Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
MLS-WATER-02          Filtering Water (2 weeks). Starts fresh (no earlier unit
                      needed).
MLS-WATER-03          Making Water Safe and Writing the Guide (3 weeks).
                      Builds on MLS-WATER-01, MLS-WATER-02.

Units that build on the same earlier unit, or start fresh, can run at the same
time.

The LSB framework functions purely as an adaptive, supportive scaffolding
framework for lesson content delivery. It does not prescribe operational
classroom routines, behavior management habits, or physical school rules.


==============================================================================
MLS-WATER-01: How Water Becomes Unsafe and Safe Storage
2 weeks. Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
==============================================================================
Goal: Builds the germ-route knowledge and covered-storage skill that the final
guide and all treatment steps rely on.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Explain that clear water can carry germs that cause
[changed: EB-09]      diarrhoea, typhoid and cholera. Ask why clear water can
                      still make us ill and how germs get in, and have
                      learners answer from the lesson. Then state the precise
                      answer with the three routes from the guide: waste,
                      dirty containers and touched hands, and write the three
                      routes on chart paper with chalk.

2. Lead Educator      Show an open bucket with a cup and a covered container
[changed: EB-04]      with a long-handled ladle like the covered jugs Flint
                      families keep for tested tap water. Explain that safely
                      collected water becomes unsafe in an open bucket or with
                      a touched cup. Have every learner hold up scrap paper
                      showing covered or open for two cases, look at all
                      answers, and re-explain if many are wrong before telling
                      learners to use covered containers and pouring or the
                      ladle.

3. Lead Educator      Each learner writes in their notebook three ways germs
                      get into drinking water and three ways to stop them.
                      Keep each learner's name on their own work only.

4. Lead Educator      Practise safe storage with the covered container and
                      ladle. Learners take turns pouring water out or lifting
                      it with the ladle, never scooping with a cup.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Covered container, long-handled ladle, chart paper and chalk are
  available.
  If not: If the ladle is missing, use pouring only and drop the ladle turn;
  combine steps 2 and 4 in one period.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-09 Elaborative Interrogation: telling the three routes
  becomes asking why clear water can make us ill, learners attempt from the
  lesson, then the precise three-route answer is stated
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: storage explanation adds
  every-learner scrap-paper answers checked during the lesson with
  re-explanation if many are wrong


==============================================================================
MLS-WATER-02: Filtering Water
2 weeks. Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
==============================================================================
Goal: Produces filtered water and the understanding that filtering alone does
not make water safe to drink.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Show pre-cut plastic bottles with cut edges covered,
                      plus cloth, fine sand, coarse sand and small stones.
                      Tell learners they must not touch the cut edges or any
                      blade.

2. Lead Educator      Demonstrate one filter by turning a bottle upside down
[changed: EB-04]      and filling it with cloth, fine sand, coarse sand and
                      small stones. Pour muddy water through and compare
                      before and after in clear cups. Have every learner hold
                      up a slate showing the layer order, look at all the
                      slates, and re-explain the order if many are wrong
                      before group work. Nothing is collected or marked.

3. Peer Facilitator   In small groups, learners fill their own pre-cut bottles
                      with the same layers and pour muddy water through. They
                      must not touch the cut edges or lift more than their own
                      bottle.

4. Lead Educator      Ask why filtered water is still not safe to drink even
[changed: EB-09]      though it looks clear, and have learners answer from the
                      lesson. Then state that filtering removes mud and some
                      dirt but not all germs, so it still needs disinfection
                      before drinking and, in Flint, testing before trusting
                      city tap water. Each group closes its filtered bottle
                      with a cover and keeps it for the next unit.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Used plastic bottles, cloth, fine sand, coarse sand and small stones
  are available.
  If not: If materials are short, the educator demonstrates one filter while
  groups observe in turn; drop repeat pours and keep one comparison.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: every learner shows the
  layer order on slates after the demonstration, answers are viewed and
  re-explained if many are wrong before group work
- Evidence-Based, EB-09 Elaborative Interrogation: telling that filtered water
  needs disinfection becomes asking why clear filtered water is still unsafe,
  then stating the precise germ reason


==============================================================================
MLS-WATER-03: Making Water Safe and Writing the Guide
3 weeks. Builds on MLS-WATER-01, MLS-WATER-02.
==============================================================================
Goal: Turns stored and filtered water into boiled and sun-treated safe water,
adds handwashing, and produces each learner's own safe-water guide.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Recall from MLS-WATER-01 and MLS-WATER-02: use the
[changed: EB-09]      covered storage chart and the filtered water bottles.
                      Ask why boiling water for one minute makes it safe, have
                      learners answer from the lesson on scrap paper, then
                      state that boiling for one minute kills germs, though
                      Flint water still needs testing for lead. Demonstrate
                      boiling water for one minute with a kettle on a stove
                      while learners observe from their seats and must not
                      touch the kettle, stove or cup.

2. Lead Educator      Lay clear plastic bottles of filtered water on a school
                      playground blacktop within reach. Leave them in full sun
                      for at least six hours. The whole class goes together
                      and the educator stays with all the groups, and learners
                      must not touch the hot bottles.

3. Lead Educator      Show the tippy-tap made from a jerrycan, stick frame and
[changed: EB-04]      string pedal. Teach the six steps of handwashing with
                      soap and running water. Ask how many handwashing steps
                      there are and have every learner show the number with
                      fingers, look at all answers, and re-explain any missed
                      steps before moving on. The whole class goes together
                      and the educator stays with all the groups, and learners
                      must not pull the stick frame or lift the jerrycan.

4. Lead Educator      Organise a rota so learners practise handwashing at the
                      tippy-tap before break. The whole class goes together
                      and the educator stays with all the groups. Learners
                      must not lift the jerrycan or pull the string.

5. Lead Educator      Give a full lesson of quiet writing time. Each learner
                      drafts a one-page guide for a younger child explaining
                      how germs get into water, two ways to make water safe
                      with one labelled drawing, and why filtering alone is
                      not enough.

6. Peer Facilitator   In pairs, learners check each other's draft against the
                      three required parts and write one note on what is
                      missing or unclear, then swap roles.

7. Lead Educator      Give a second full lesson for each learner to correct
                      the draft using the partner's note and write a final
                      copy.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Sunny ground-level space, kettle with stove, soap, jerrycan with
  sticks and string are available.
  If not: If sun is weak, shorten SODIS to a demonstration setup and use
  boiled water for the guide; if a week is lost, combine handwashing practice
  into one period and keep both writing lessons.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-09 Elaborative Interrogation: boiling explanation becomes
  ask-then-tell with precise why question, learner attempt, then stated answer
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: handwashing teaching adds
  every-learner finger answer checked during the lesson with re-explain before
  moving on
```
