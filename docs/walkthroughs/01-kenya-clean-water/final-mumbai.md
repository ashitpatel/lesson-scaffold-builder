==============================================================================
LESSON SCAFFOLD: Grade 6 Science and Technology - Strand 3: Clean Water and
Health
==============================================================================
Goal: Learners explain how water becomes unsafe and describe and demonstrate
safe ways to treat and store drinking water.
Grade band: Primary. Period: 45 minutes.

UNIT                  WHAT IT IS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MLS-WATER-01          Why Water Can Make Us Ill and Filtering (2 weeks).
                      Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
MLS-WATER-02          Making Water Safe and Keeping It Clean (2 weeks). Starts
                      fresh (no earlier unit needed).
MLS-WATER-03          Writing a Safe-Water Guide (2 weeks). Builds on
                      MLS-WATER-01, MLS-WATER-02.

Units that build on the same earlier unit, or start fresh, can run at the same
time.

The LSB framework functions purely as an adaptive, supportive scaffolding
framework for lesson content delivery. It does not prescribe operational
classroom routines, behavior management habits, or physical school rules.


==============================================================================
MLS-WATER-01: Why Water Can Make Us Ill and Filtering
2 weeks. Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
==============================================================================
Goal: Builds the germ-route and filter-limit knowledge that safe-treatment
choices and the final guide rely on.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Explain that clear water can carry germs from waste,
[changed: EB-04]      dirty containers, and touched hands, as in water stored
                      in overhead tanks and buckets. Every learner writes
                      three ways germs get into drinking water on scrap paper
                      and holds it up at the same time. Look at all answers
                      and re-explain any way many missed before moving on,
                      then learners copy the class list into notebooks using
                      chalk and chart paper.

2. Lead Educator      Using a plastic bottle the educator cut before the
                      lesson, show layers of cloth, fine sand, coarse sand,
                      and small stones. Pour muddy water through and compare
                      water before and after in two clear reused bottles, such
                      as cold-drink bottles kept in flats. Learners must not
                      drink the muddy or filtered water.

3. Lead Educator      Give each group a bottle the educator has already cut
                      plus piles of cloth, fine sand, coarse sand, and small
                      stones. Groups fill the layers in order and filter muddy
                      water into a reused cup. Learners must not pick up
                      broken plastic edges or drink the water.

4. Lead Educator      Ask why filtered water still needs disinfecting before
[changed: EB-09]      drinking and have every learner write one line from the
                      lesson. Then state that filtering removes mud and some
                      dirt but not all germs, so germs remain. Learners write
                      in notebooks why filtered water still needs disinfecting
                      before drinking.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Cloth, fine sand, coarse sand, small stones, used plastic bottles,
  muddy water, chalk, and chart paper are available.
  If not: Use one educator-made filter for the demo, combine groups for the
  build, and drop the second before-after comparison to stay in 2 weeks.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: every learner answers at
  the same time on scrap paper, educator looks at all answers and re-explains
  misses before copying
- Evidence-Based, EB-09 Elaborative Interrogation: telling the filter limit
  becomes asking why filtered water still needs disinfecting, learners attempt
  first, then precise answer is stated


==============================================================================
MLS-WATER-02: Making Water Safe and Keeping It Clean
2 weeks. Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
==============================================================================
Goal: Provides the boiling, sunlight, handwashing, and storage practices that
the final guide describes.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Demonstrate boiling water for one minute using a kettle
[changed: EB-04]      on a stove while learners watch from their seats. Show
                      the steam and explain boiling kills germs. Every learner
                      at the same time holds up scrap paper answering how long
                      to boil, you look at all answers and re-explain if many
                      are wrong before moving on. Learners must not touch the
                      kettle, stove, or hot cup.

2. Lead Educator      Take the whole class together to lay clear bottles of
                      filtered water on a sunny podium corner by the
                      classroom, with the educator staying with all groups.
                      Explain the bottles need at least six hours in full sun.
                      Learners must not drink the water before the time is up
                      or pick up sharp objects.

3. Lead Educator      Bring the whole class together to the tippy-tap made
[changed: CL-02]      from a jerrycan, sticks, and string, with the educator
                      staying with all groups. Teach the six steps of
                      handwashing with soap and running water and let groups
                      practise in turn with one learner showing the steps and
                      one learner checking the steps on fingers, then they
                      swap roles. Learners must not lift the full jerrycan.

4. Lead Educator      Show a covered container such as a steel matka or drum
[changed: EB-09]      used in society flats and demonstrate pouring water out
                      or taking it with a long-handled ladle. Ask why scooping
                      with a touched cup makes water unsafe, every learner
                      answers from the lesson, then state that hands put germs
                      into the water so it must never be scooped with a
                      touched cup. Learners copy the two storage rules into
                      notebooks.

5. Lead Educator      In the classroom, with the educator present, make one
[changed: CL-04]      class chart on chart paper worded like a housing-society
                      notice-board note in Marathi and Hindi of the two safe
                      storage rules. Each group says its two rules aloud and
                      the educator writes them as one class list with no names
                      or marks.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Fuel and stove for one boiling demo, a sunny flat spot, soap, water,
  jerrycan, sticks, string, and a covered container with ladle are available.
  If not: Replace the demo with an explanation of the one-minute boil, use one
  sunny day only, and shorten practise turns or drop the repeat practise to
  stay in 2 weeks.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: every learner answers
  boiling-time on scrap at the same time, educator looks and re-explains if
  many are wrong
- Evidence-Based, EB-09 Elaborative Interrogation: storage telling becomes
  why-question learners attempt, then precise germ answer is stated
- Connected Learning, CL-02 Structural Peer Roles: added rotating
  shower-checker pair roles to handwashing practice
- Connected Learning, CL-04 Collective Shared Purpose: combined individual
  storage rules into one class chart with no names or marks


==============================================================================
MLS-WATER-03: Writing a Safe-Water Guide
2 weeks. Builds on MLS-WATER-01, MLS-WATER-02.
==============================================================================
Goal: Turns germ routes, filter limits, and safe treatments into each
learner's own one-page guide with a labelled drawing, revised after partner
check.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Recall from MLS-WATER-01 and MLS-WATER-02: use the class
                      lists of germ routes, filter limits, and safe
                      treatments. Each learner chooses two safe methods and
                      plans a one-page guide for a younger child in their
                      notebook.

2. Lead Educator      Give a full lesson of quiet writing time. Each learner
                      drafts a one-page guide explaining how germs get into
                      water, two ways to make it safe and why filtering alone
                      is not enough, with one labelled drawing.

3. Peer Facilitator   In pairs, read each other's draft and check it covers
                      germ routes, two safe methods, filter limits, and a
                      labelled drawing. Each partner writes one note on what
                      is missing or unclear, then partners swap roles.

4. Lead Educator      Give a second full lesson for each learner to correct
                      the draft using the partner's note and write a final
                      copy. Collect the guides in class with each learner's
                      name on their own work.

5. Lead Educator      Arrange a 10-minute session with another class in the
[changed: CL-05]      classroom, with the educator present. Pairs take turns
                      showing their final guides to a small group of listeners
                      and reading the two safe methods aloud. Showing is a
                      choice: a learner may lay the guide on the desk for
                      listeners to read, or keep it and just listen.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Notebooks and pencils for planning, drafting, and final copies are
  available.
  If not: Draft on reused paper, combine planning and drafting in one period,
  and shorten revision time to stay in 2 weeks.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Connected Learning, CL-05 Real Audience Production: final guides shown and
  read to another class as a real audience
