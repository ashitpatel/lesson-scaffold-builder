==============================================================================
LESSON SCAFFOLD: Grade 5 Science - How Plants Grow
==============================================================================
Goal: Understand what plants need to grow and how leaves, stems, roots and
seeds help plants grow.
Grade band: Primary. Period: 45 minutes.

UNIT                  WHAT IT IS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MLS-PLANT-01          What Plants Need and Why Leaves Are Green and Flat (2
                      weeks). Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
MLS-PLANT-02          Light for Growth and How Stems Carry Water (3 weeks).
                      Builds on MLS-PLANT-01.
MLS-PLANT-03          Roots, Seeds and Leaves from Nearby Grounds (2 weeks).
                      Builds on MLS-PLANT-01.

Units that build on the same earlier unit, or start fresh, can run at the same
time.

The LSB framework functions purely as an adaptive, supportive scaffolding
framework for lesson content delivery. It does not prescribe operational
classroom routines, behavior management habits, or physical school rules.


==============================================================================
MLS-PLANT-01: What Plants Need and Why Leaves Are Green and Flat
2 weeks. Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
==============================================================================
Goal: Establishes the four things plants need and how leaf colour and shape
catch light, which later units test and use.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Explain on chart paper that plants need light, water,
[changed: EB-04]      air and minerals from soil as in the small amount of
                      potting mix in corridor pots. Pupils copy the four-item
                      list into notebooks. Then ask which of the four is
                      missing from two examples and have every pupil hold up
                      the answer on scrap paper at the same time, look at all
                      answers, and re-explain any missing need before moving
                      on.

2. Lead Educator      Ask why leaves are green and why flat leaves catch more
[changed: EB-09]      light like kangkong and bayam grown in corridor pots,
                      and have every pupil write a short answer on scrap paper
                      from the lesson. Then state the precise answers that
                      green comes from chlorophyll and flat shape catches
                      light, and show both on chart paper. Each pupil checks
                      their own answer against the chart. Nothing is collected
                      or marked.

3. Lead Educator      Give a full lesson of quiet writing time. Each student
                      writes in their notebook why leaves are green and why
                      flat leaves catch light.

4. Lead Educator      Make a class chart on chart paper listing light, water,
                      air and minerals. Keep the chart for later units to
                      check against.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Chart paper, chalk and student notebooks are available.
  If not: Reuse one chart for both the four needs and leaf notes, combine
  steps 1 and 2 in one period, and drop the spare copy task.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: every pupil answers at
  once on scrap paper about the four needs, educator looks at all answers and
  re-explains before moving on
- Evidence-Based, EB-09 Elaborative Interrogation: telling becomes
  asking-then-telling with precise why questions on green and flat leaves,
  pupil attempt first then precise answers stated and self-checked


==============================================================================
MLS-PLANT-02: Light for Growth and How Stems Carry Water
3 weeks. Builds on MLS-PLANT-01.
==============================================================================
Goal: Tests the need for light with a shared classroom comparison and shows
stems carrying water up from roots.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Recall from MLS-PLANT-01: use the class chart of light,
                      water, air and minerals. Place one potted seedling in a
                      small HDB corridor pot in classroom light and one in a
                      classroom cupboard with the teacher, and pupils observe
                      both together.

2. Peer Facilitator   In mixed groups of three with the educator present, take
[changed: CL-02]      the roles of Measurer, Recorder and Checker: the
                      Measurer measures both seedlings with reused string, the
                      Recorder marks lengths on the class totals chart on
                      chart paper, and the Checker checks the marks. Groups
                      rotate roles each day. Record one class total each day
                      without ranking pupils.

3. Lead Educator      Place a white flower such as a white orchid in a cup of
[changed: EB-04]      dyed water at the front of the class. Pupils watch from
                      their seats over two days and copy the sentence Stems
                      carry water up from the roots once. Each pupil holds up
                      a slate at the same time to show where the colour will
                      go next, the educator looks at all slates and
                      re-explains if many are wrong before moving on.

4. Lead Educator      Give a full lesson of quiet writing time. Each student
                      writes in their notebook what happened to both seedlings
                      and how stems carry water up from roots.

5. Lead Educator      In week 3, pupils answer three short questions from
[changed: EB-01]      memory on scrap paper about light for seedlings and
                      stems carrying water, with the chart and notebooks put
                      away. Then the educator shows the answers so each pupil
                      checks their own. Nothing is collected or marked.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Two potted seedlings, two cups, water and access to a classroom
  cupboard are available.
  If not: Use one seedling in light then covered for two days, combine
  measuring into one period, and drop the second observation day.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: every pupil shows
  predicted colour movement on slates at once, educator checks all and
  re-explains if many are wrong
- Evidence-Based, EB-01 Retrieval Practice: low-stakes recall from memory in
  week 3 with chart put away, answers shown straight after, nothing collected
- Connected Learning, CL-02 Structural Peer Roles: converted alone tracking
  into rotating Measurer, Recorder and Checker roles for the existing seedling
  measurement


==============================================================================
MLS-PLANT-03: Roots, Seeds and Leaves from Nearby Grounds
2 weeks. Builds on MLS-PLANT-01.
==============================================================================
Goal: Adds how roots take in water with minerals and gives each student sorted
seeds and fallen leaves to link parts to growth.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Recall from MLS-PLANT-01: use the class chart of light,
[changed: EB-04]      water, air and minerals. Explain on chart paper that
                      roots grow down in shallow corridor pots where space is
                      tight and take in water mixed with minerals for leaves.
                      Ask where water with minerals goes and have every pupil
                      hold up a scrap-paper answer, look at all answers, and
                      re-explain if many are wrong before moving on.

2. Lead Educator      Walk with the whole class together around the HDB school
                      grounds with the educator staying with all groups to
                      collect fallen seeds into one shared basket. Pupils must
                      not pick up thorny plants, touch insects, or lift heavy
                      branches.

3. Lead Educator      Walk with the whole class together on the HDB school
                      grounds with the educator staying with all groups to
                      collect three different fallen leaves each. Pupils must
                      not touch water plants, pick up sharp objects, or lift
                      heavy stones, and bring leaves back in the shared
                      basket.

4. Lead Educator      Sort the shared seeds by size on desks using the shared
                      basket. Each student draws one seed in their notebook
                      with quiet drawing time.

5. Lead Educator      Count the sorted seeds in each size group together and
[changed: CL-04]      write one class total for each size on chart paper. Use
                      totals only with no names or marks. Keep the chart in
                      the classroom.

6. Lead Educator      Give a full lesson of quiet writing time. Each student
                      writes answers in their notebook to why roots grow down
                      and how water gets from roots to leaves.

7. Lead Educator      In week 2, open a lesson with four short questions on
[changed: EB-01]      roots and leaves answered from memory on scrap paper,
                      with the chart, notebooks and books put away. Show the
                      answers straight after so each pupil checks their own.
                      Nothing is collected, marked or recorded.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Dry weather and access to school grounds for short walks.
  If not: Use fallen leaves already on school grounds near the classroom, then
  combine sorting and drawing in one period.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: added whole-class
  scrap-paper check with educator review and re-explain before moving on
- Evidence-Based, EB-01 Retrieval Practice: low-stakes week-2 recall of roots
  and leaves from memory, self-checked, nothing collected
- Connected Learning, CL-04 Collective Shared Purpose: added class totals for
  each seed-size group on chart paper
