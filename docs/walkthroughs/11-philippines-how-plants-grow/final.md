==============================================================================
LESSON SCAFFOLD: Grade 5 Science - How Plants Grow
==============================================================================
Goal: Understand how plants grow, what plants need to grow, and how leaves,
roots, stems and seeds work.
Grade band: Primary. Period: 45 minutes.

UNIT                  WHAT IT IS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MLS-GROW-01           What Plants Need and Why Leaves Are Green and Flat (2
                      weeks). Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
MLS-GROW-02           Light and Growth Classroom Experiment (3 weeks). Builds
                      on MLS-GROW-01.
MLS-GROW-03           Roots and Stems Carry Water and Minerals (2 weeks).
                      Builds on MLS-GROW-01.
MLS-GROW-04           Seeds and Leaves Around the School (2 weeks). Builds on
                      MLS-GROW-01.

Units that build on the same earlier unit, or start fresh, can run at the same
time.

The LSB framework functions purely as an adaptive, supportive scaffolding
framework for lesson content delivery. It does not prescribe operational
classroom routines, behavior management habits, or physical school rules.


==============================================================================
MLS-GROW-01: What Plants Need and Why Leaves Are Green and Flat
2 weeks. Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
==============================================================================
Goal: Builds the shared list of four plant needs and the leaf explanation that
later experiments and observations use.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Explain using chalk on chart paper that plants need
[changed: EB-04]      light, water, air and minerals from soil. Pupils copy
                      the four-item list into notebooks. Every pupil holds up
                      a slate answering two uses the educator names, the
                      educator looks at all slates and re-explains any need
                      many miss or moves on. Keep the chart for later units.

2. Lead Educator      Ask why leaves are green and why flat leaves catch more
[changed: EB-09]      light, and each pupil writes an answer from the textbook
                      paragraph in notebooks. State that leaves are green
                      because of chlorophyll and flat to catch light, pointing
                      to where the paragraph says so. Pupils correct their own
                      answers against what is stated, with nothing collected.

3. Lead Educator      Give quiet time for each pupil to write two sentences
                      explaining what plants need and how leaves help. Each
                      pupil keeps the writing in their own notebook. Use the
                      class chart only, with no extra materials.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Chalk, chart paper and notebooks are available for the needs list and
  leaf answers.
  If not: Use the chalkboard for the list and have pupils copy into any paper;
  if a week is lost, combine steps 2 and 3 into one period and drop the second
  sentence.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: every pupil answers on
  slates during the needs explanation, educator looks at all answers and
  re-explains or moves on
- Evidence-Based, EB-09 Elaborative Interrogation: precise why questions
  attempted from the paragraph first, then the educator states both leaf
  answers for self-checking


==============================================================================
MLS-GROW-02: Light and Growth Classroom Experiment
3 weeks. Builds on MLS-GROW-01.
==============================================================================
Goal: Tests the light need by comparing covered and uncovered shared seedlings
and recording class growth totals.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Recall from MLS-GROW-01: use the class chart of light,
[changed: EB-04]      water, air and minerals. Place two shared seedlings in
                      reused pots at the front, one covered with a reused box
                      and one uncovered. Every pupil holds up a slate showing
                      covered or uncovered for which seedling will grow
                      taller, the educator looks at all slates and re-explains
                      light need if many are wrong before moving on. Pupils
                      copy the setup into notebooks.

2. Lead Educator      Observe both seedlings together each lesson over two
[changed: CL-02]      weeks. In mixed pairs one pupil is Measurer with paper
                      strips and the other is Recorder, then they swap roles
                      each lesson, and the educator marks class totals on
                      chart paper. Each pupil draws one seedling in their own
                      notebook.

3. Lead Educator      Give quiet time for each pupil to write three sentences
[changed: EB-09]      explaining what happened to the covered seedling and
                      why. Keep the writing in their own notebook. Ask why the
                      covered seedling stayed short and pale, hear pupils'
                      answers, then state that it missed light so it could not
                      grow well, and discuss totals as a class using the
                      chart.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Two shared seedlings in reused pots and a reused box cover are
  available in the classroom.
  If not: Use one shared seedling with part covered by folded paper; if a week
  is lost, shorten to one week of observations and drop one drawing, keeping
  class totals on the chart.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: every pupil answers on
  slates which seedling will grow taller, educator looks at all slates and
  re-explains if many are wrong
- Evidence-Based, EB-09 Elaborative Interrogation: precise why-question about
  the covered seedling attempted first, then precise missed-light answer
  stated before class discussion
- Connected Learning, CL-02 Structural Peer Roles: named rotating Measurer and
  Recorder pairs for the existing height tracking


==============================================================================
MLS-GROW-03: Roots and Stems Carry Water and Minerals
2 weeks. Builds on MLS-GROW-01.
==============================================================================
Goal: Explains how roots take in water mixed with minerals and how stems carry
it up to the leaves.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Recall from MLS-GROW-01: use the class chart of light,
[changed: EB-04]      water, air and minerals. Explain that roots grow down
                      into soil and take in water mixed with minerals. Every
                      pupil holds up a slate answer to two questions on this,
                      the educator looks at all slates and re-explains what
                      many miss before moving on. Pupils copy the two
                      sentences into notebooks.

2. Lead Educator      Place a white flower in a reused bottle of coloured
                      water at the front of the class. Pupils watch from seats
                      over two days. Each pupil draws the flower once each day
                      in their own notebook.

3. Lead Educator      Give quiet time for each pupil to write answers in
[changed: EB-09]      notebooks to why roots grow down and how water gets from
                      roots to leaves. Use the flower observation and the
                      class chart. The educator then states the precise
                      answers for both questions. Keep the writing in their
                      own notebook.

4. Lead Educator      Build one shared labelled diagram on chart paper showing
[changed: CL-04]      water moving from roots through the stem to the flower.
                      Each pupil adds one arrow or label from their notebook
                      drawing, without names or marks. Keep the chart for
                      later units.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: A white flower, a reused bottle and coloured water are available at
  the front of the class.
  If not: Use any cut stem with leaves in plain water in a reused jar; if a
  day is lost, observe over one day and combine steps 2 and 3 in one period.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: every pupil answers on
  slates during the root explanation, educator looks and re-explains before
  moving on
- Evidence-Based, EB-09 Elaborative Interrogation: pupils first attempt the
  why/how answers then educator states both precise answers
- Connected Learning, CL-04 Collective Shared Purpose: combined individual
  flower drawings into one shared class diagram of the water path


==============================================================================
MLS-GROW-04: Seeds and Leaves Around the School
2 weeks. Builds on MLS-GROW-01.
==============================================================================
Goal: Builds recognition of seed and leaf variety through supervised
collecting, sorting and drawing in the school grounds.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Recall from MLS-GROW-01: use the class chart of what
                      plants need. The whole class goes together with the
                      educator around the school grounds and collects fallen
                      seeds into one shared basket. The educator stays with
                      all groups; pupils must not pick up sharp objects or
                      lift heavy branches.

2. Lead Educator      Sort the shared seeds by size on desks. Each pupil draws
                      one seed in their own notebook. Return all seeds to the
                      shared basket.

3. Lead Educator      The whole class goes together with the educator around
                      the school grounds and collects three different fallen
                      leaves per pupil into the shared basket. The educator
                      stays with all groups; pupils must not touch thorny
                      plants or pick up sharp objects. Back inside, sort
                      leaves by shape on desks.

4. Lead Educator      Give quiet time for each pupil to draw two different
                      leaves and write one sentence about each shape in their
                      notebook. Keep the work in their own notebook.

5. Lead Educator      Place one sheet of chart paper on desks for a shared
[changed: CL-04]      class chart of leaf shapes. Each pupil copies one leaf
                      shape from their notebook onto the chart, without names
                      or marks. A pupil may keep the drawing in the notebook
                      instead. Keep all notebook drawings in pupils' own
                      notebooks.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Access to school grounds in dry weather and one shared basket for
  fallen seeds and leaves.
  If not: Collect from the classroom edge or use brought fallen seeds and
  leaves already on site; if a week is lost, do one outdoor walk instead of
  two and sort both seeds and leaves indoors in one period.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Connected Learning, CL-04 Collective Shared Purpose: combined leaf drawings
  into a shared class shape chart
