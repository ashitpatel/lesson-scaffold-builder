==============================================================================
LESSON SCAFFOLD: Grade 5 Science - How Plants Grow
==============================================================================
Goal: Understand how plants grow, including what plants need and how leaves,
roots, and stems support growth.
Grade band: Primary. Period: 45 minutes.

UNIT                  WHAT IT IS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MLS-PLANT-01          What Plants Need and How Light Matters (2 weeks). Starts
                      fresh (no earlier unit needed).
MLS-PLANT-02          Leaves Are Green and Flat (2 weeks). Builds on
                      MLS-PLANT-01.
MLS-PLANT-03          Roots, Stems, and Seeds (3 weeks). Builds on
                      MLS-PLANT-01.

Units that build on the same earlier unit, or start fresh, can run at the same
time.

The LSB framework functions purely as an adaptive, supportive scaffolding
framework for lesson content delivery. It does not prescribe operational
classroom routines, behavior management habits, or physical school rules.


==============================================================================
MLS-PLANT-01: What Plants Need and How Light Matters
2 weeks. Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
==============================================================================
Goal: Establishes the four needs and produces shared classroom evidence that
light affects growth for later leaf, root, and stem work.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Explain that plants need light, water, air, and minerals
[changed: EB-04]      from soil called daw', maa', hawaa', and ma'aadin in
                      Arabic. Pupils repeat the four needs and copy the list
                      into notebooks. Each pupil holds up a reused-paper card
                      naming one need you call out, you look at all cards and
                      re-explain any need many miss before moving on. Keep the
                      class list on chart paper for later units.

2. Lead Educator      Place two shared seedlings in reused cups with soil
                      watered here by small measured cupfuls, drop by drop at
                      the front of the class. Keep one in light and cover one
                      with a reused box. Pupils watch from their seats and
                      leave both in the classroom.

3. Peer Facilitator   In pairs, look at the two shared seedlings during class
[changed: CL-02]      and mark the class table on chart paper. One partner
                      observes and describes while the other marks, then they
                      swap roles each day. Use only totals for the class, with
                      no individual names or scores. Return to seats after
                      marking.

4. Lead Educator      Ask why the covered seedling grew differently and have
[changed: EB-09]      each pupil write one attempt in their notebook from the
                      lesson. Compare the two seedlings with the class and
                      state that light affects growth which matters for the
                      food our school garden can provide. Keep the chart for
                      MLS-PLANT-02 and MLS-PLANT-03.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Two shared seedlings, reused cups, soil, one reused cover box, chart
  paper and notebooks are available.
  If not: Use one shared school plant and a covered part with paper; if a week
  is lost, combine observation marking and comparison in one period and drop
  the second observation round.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: every pupil answers on
  reused-paper cards at the same time, educator looks at all answers and
  re-explains before moving on
- Evidence-Based, EB-09 Elaborative Interrogation: precise why question
  attempted by each pupil first, then the precise answer that light affects
  growth is stated
- Connected Learning, CL-02 Structural Peer Roles: added observer and marker
  roles with daily swap for the existing class table marking


==============================================================================
MLS-PLANT-02: Leaves Are Green and Flat
2 weeks. Builds on MLS-PLANT-01.
==============================================================================
Goal: Links leaf color and flat shape to catching light, using collected
leaves to show how leaves support growth.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Recall from MLS-PLANT-01: use the class chart of light,
[changed: EB-04]      water, air, and minerals. Explain that leaves are green
                      from chlorophyll and flat to catch light like mint,
                      sage, and zaatar in school containers. Ask why leaves
                      are green and flat and have every pupil write the answer
                      on scrap and hold it up at the same time, then look at
                      all answers and re-explain if many are wrong before
                      moving on.

2. Lead Educator      Go outside as a whole class with the educator staying
                      with all groups to the edge of a nearby water body and
                      school grounds. Collect only fallen leaves into one
                      shared basket. Near water and plants, pupils must not
                      touch water, must not pick up litter, and must not lift
                      heavy objects.

3. Peer Facilitator   Back inside, sort the shared leaves by shape on desks in
[changed: CL-02]      pairs with roles of Sorter and Recorder, then swap roles
                      halfway. Each pupil draws one leaf in their notebook and
                      writes one label for its shape. Keep names only on their
                      own work.

4. Lead Educator      Compare two leaves and ask why flat shape helps both
[changed: EB-09]      catch light, having pupils answer from the lesson. State
                      that both stay flat to catch light. Each pupil writes
                      one sentence linking flat shape to light from
                      MLS-PLANT-01. Keep the drawings in notebooks.

5. Lead Educator      In the classroom, place each pair's leaf drawing onto
[changed: CL-04]      one shared class chart of leaf shapes on chart paper,
                      without names or scores. Keep the chart for
                      MLS-PLANT-03.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Dry weather allows a short whole-class walk to collect fallen leaves
  near school grounds or a nearby water body.
  If not: Skip the walk and sort leaves already fallen in school grounds from
  one quick pickup, or use the prior shared basket; drop the final comparison
  sentence to fit the declared weeks.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: every pupil answers why
  leaves are green and flat on scrap at the same time, educator looks at all
  answers and re-explains if many are wrong
- Evidence-Based, EB-09 Elaborative Interrogation: asking-then-telling for why
  flat shape helps catch light before pupils write the linking sentence
- Connected Learning, CL-02 Structural Peer Roles: added Sorter and Recorder
  roles with halfway swap to existing pair sort
- Connected Learning, CL-04 Collective Shared Purpose: combined pair drawings
  into one shared class leaf-shape chart


==============================================================================
MLS-PLANT-03: Roots, Stems, and Seeds
3 weeks. Builds on MLS-PLANT-01.
==============================================================================
Goal: Shows how roots take in water with minerals, stems carry water upward,
and seeds vary, completing the growth picture.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Recall from MLS-PLANT-01: use the class chart of light,
[changed: EB-04]      water, air, and minerals. Explain that roots grow down
                      into soil and take in water with minerals. Every pupil
                      answers one question on scrap paper at the same time and
                      holds it up, look at all answers and re-explain if many
                      are wrong. Pupils copy the two questions about roots and
                      stems into notebooks.

2. Lead Educator      Walk as a whole class with the educator staying with all
                      groups around the school grounds. Collect only fallen
                      seeds into one shared basket. Near plants, pupils must
                      not touch living plants, must not pick up litter, and
                      must not lift heavy objects.

3. Lead Educator      Back inside, sort the shared seeds by size on desks
                      using lentils, chickpeas, and fava beans. Each pupil
                      draws one seed in their notebook. Keep names only on
                      their own work.

4. Lead Educator      Place one white flower in a reused cup of colored water
[changed: EB-08]      at the front of the class. Pupils watch from their seats
                      over two days. In pairs, each pupil says why the colour
                      moves up into the flower. State that stems carry water
                      up from roots.

5. Lead Educator      Give quiet writing time for each pupil to write answers
                      to why roots grow down and how water reaches leaves.
                      Pupils use the roots explanation and stem change they
                      saw. Keep the answers in notebooks.

6. Lead Educator      Make one class seed table on chart paper at the front of
[changed: CL-04]      the classroom with the educator present. Each pair adds
                      one mark for their seed size, using only class totals
                      with no names or scores. Look together at the table to
                      see how seeds vary.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Fallen seeds are present in school grounds and one white flower with
  a reused cup is available for two days.
  If not: Use a smaller shared seed sample and shorten stem watching to one
  day; if a week is lost, combine seed sorting with drawing in one period and
  drop one practice question.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: every pupil answers at the
  same time on scrap paper held up, answers are looked at and re-explained if
  many are wrong
- Evidence-Based, EB-08 Self-Explanation: pairs say why the colour moves up
  the stem, then the stem-carries-water reasoning is stated
- Connected Learning, CL-04 Collective Shared Purpose: combined individual
  seed drawings into one class seed table showing variation
