# Ngữ văn 6 (Literature, Grade 6) - Unit 2: Folk Tales and Retelling

*Teacher's book following the 2018 General Education Programme of the Ministry of Education and Training (MOET). Suggested length: 6 weeks of 45-minute lessons.*

## 2.1 What a folk tale is

A folk tale is a story passed down by word of mouth for many generations before anyone wrote it down. Folk tales usually have a simple structure: a beginning that introduces a character and a problem, a middle with a series of events (often in threes), and an ending in which the problem is solved and good is rewarded. Characters are often types rather than individuals: the kind youngest child, the greedy elder brother, the clever animal. Many folk tales contain a magical helper or object.

Pupils should be able to name the three parts of a folk tale and recognise the character types.

## 2.2 Folk tales of our nation

Open the unit with a lecture on the history of collecting folk tales in Vietnam, from oral storytellers in villages to the collections published in the twentieth century, and on how folk tales were told during Tết. Pupils copy the list of famous collectors and dates into their notebooks. Spend two lessons on this lecture.

## 2.3 Reading two folk tales

Pupils read two folk tales from the textbook: one about a poor fisherman who is given a magic pot and loses it through greed, and one about a clever rabbit who escapes a tiger by trickery. For each tale, the teacher reads it aloud first, then pupils read it silently. Pupils then fill in a story map: characters, problem, three main events, and ending.

## 2.4 Character and lesson

Pupils discuss what kind of person each main character is, using evidence from the story (what the character says and does). Each pupil writes two sentences about the lesson each story teaches, using a quotation from the text to support it.

## 2.5 Retelling aloud

Pupils practise retelling one of the tales in their own words in under three minutes, without the book, using only their story map. Pupils who retell best are chosen to perform at the school's Tết celebration, and their names are announced on the school loudspeaker.

Teachers should record each pupil's retelling on a phone and upload the videos to the class Zalo group so that parents can see them.

## 2.6 Collecting a tale from home

Each pupil asks a grandparent or older neighbour to tell them a folk tale, and writes it down. Pupils should visit elderly neighbours they don't know well if their own family has no storyteller.

## 2.7 Culminating task

Each pupil writes their own folk tale of 400 to 600 words, with a beginning, a middle with three events, and an ending, and with at least one character type from the unit. Pupils give two full lessons to drafting, then read a partner's draft and suggest one improvement, then revise and write a final copy. The best tales will be printed in a class book sold at the school fair for 20,000 VND each, with pupils collecting the money.

The goal of the unit is for pupils to recognise the structure and character types of folk tales, to retell a tale in their own words, and to write a tale of their own.
