==============================================================================
LESSON SCAFFOLD: Literature Grade 6 - Unit 2: Folk Tales and Retelling
==============================================================================
Goal: Pupils recognise the structure and character types of folk tales, retell
a tale in their own words, and write a tale of their own.
Grade band: Middle. Period: 45 minutes.

UNIT                  WHAT IT IS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MLS-FOLKTALE-01       Reading and Mapping Two Folk Tales (2 weeks). Starts
                      fresh (no earlier unit needed).
MLS-FOLKTALE-02       Character, Lesson and Retelling Aloud (2 weeks). Builds
                      on MLS-FOLKTALE-01.
MLS-FOLKTALE-03       Collecting and Writing a Folk Tale (3 weeks). Builds on
                      MLS-FOLKTALE-01.

Units that build on the same earlier unit, or start fresh, can run at the same
time.

The LSB framework functions purely as an adaptive, supportive scaffolding
framework for lesson content delivery. It does not prescribe operational
classroom routines, behavior management habits, or physical school rules.


==============================================================================
MLS-FOLKTALE-01: Reading and Mapping Two Folk Tales
2 weeks. Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
==============================================================================
Goal: Builds recognition of the three-part structure, character types and
magical helpers through story maps of both tales.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Explain a folk tale in three parts: beginning with
[changed: EB-04]      character and problem, middle with three events, ending
                      where the problem is solved. Name the character types
                      and magical helpers, using chalk and board with
                      talking-drum rhythms marking beginning, middle, and
                      ending. Pupils copy the three parts and types into
                      notebooks. Then every pupil holds up a slate naming the
                      three parts, you look at all slates, and re-explain any
                      part many miss before moving on.

2. Lead Educator      Read aloud the tale of the poor fisherman with the magic
                      pot using Yoruba and English names as families use them.
                      Pupils read it silently after you. Each pupil fills a
                      story map in a notebook: characters, problem, three
                      events, ending.

3. Lead Educator      Read aloud the tale of the clever rabbit escaping the
                      tiger. Pupils read it silently after you. Each pupil
                      fills a second story map: characters, problem, three
                      events, ending.

4. Lead Educator      In week 2, ask four short questions on the three parts,
[changed: EB-01]      character types and magical helpers, answered from
                      memory on slates with books and notebooks put away. Then
                      show the answers on the board so each pupil checks their
                      own. Nothing is collected or marked.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Textbook tales, notebooks, chalk and board available for 2 weeks
  If not: If one week is lost, combine the two tale maps into shared class
  maps and drop the second silent re-read
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: every pupil answers on
  slates during the explanation, educator looks at all answers and re-explains
  before moving on
- Evidence-Based, EB-01 Retrieval Practice: low-stakes week-2 recall of
  structure, types and helpers from memory, self-checked, nothing collected


==============================================================================
MLS-FOLKTALE-02: Character, Lesson and Retelling Aloud
2 weeks. Builds on MLS-FOLKTALE-01.
==============================================================================
Goal: Turns the story maps into evidence-based character judgements, stated
lessons, and an unaided three-minute retelling.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Recall from MLS-FOLKTALE-01: use the two story maps.
[changed: EB-08]      Discuss what kind of person each main character is,
                      using what each says and does as evidence. Pupils
                      underline one action and one speech line in their
                      notebooks. In pairs, each pupil says why those lines
                      show that trait, then the educator states the precise
                      reasoning.

2. Lead Educator      Each pupil writes two sentences about the lesson each
                      story teaches. Each lesson statement includes one short
                      quotation from the tale. Work stays in notebooks with
                      the pupil's own name only.

3. Peer Facilitator   Practise retelling one tale in own words without the
                      book, using only the story map. Keep each retelling
                      under three minutes in pairs taking turns. Use notebooks
                      and story maps only, with no devices.

4. Lead Educator      Each pupil retells the chosen tale aloud to the class
                      from the story map with respect for its lesson, without
                      parody. Keep all retellings in the classroom. Use no
                      recording and no uploads.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Class time for paired practice and in-class retellings
  If not: If time is lost, shorten practice to one turn per pupil and combine
  character discussion with lesson sentences in one period
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-08 Self-Explanation: pupils explain in pairs why the
  underlined action and speech line show the trait, then the educator states
  the precise reasoning


==============================================================================
MLS-FOLKTALE-03: Collecting and Writing a Folk Tale
3 weeks. Builds on MLS-FOLKTALE-01.
==============================================================================
Goal: Converts the three-part structure and character types into each pupil's
own drafted, peer-checked and revised 400 to 600 word tale.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Recall from MLS-FOLKTALE-01: use the three-part
                      structure and character types. Each pupil asks a family
                      elder at home to tell a short folk tale only if the
                      elder is willing, such as a familiar Ijapa tale. Each
                      pupil writes the tale in a notebook and brings it to
                      class. Do not visit unfamiliar homes.

2. Lead Educator      Each pupil plans a folk tale of 400 to 600 words with a
                      beginning, a middle with three events, and an ending.
                      Include at least one character type from the unit such
                      as a trickster like Ijapa. Write the plan in a notebook.

3. Lead Educator      Give a full lesson of quiet writing time for pupils to
                      draft their tale in notebooks. Each draft follows the
                      plan with three middle events. Keep all work offline
                      with reused paper and pencils.

4. Lead Educator      Give a second full lesson of quiet writing time to
                      finish the draft. Each pupil completes the beginning,
                      three events, and ending. Keep writing in notebooks
                      only.

5. Peer Facilitator   In pairs, read a partner's draft and suggest one
                      improvement tied to structure or character type. Write
                      the suggestion on the draft. Then swap roles.

6. Lead Educator      Give class time for each pupil to revise the draft using
                      the partner's suggestion. Each pupil writes a final copy
                      in a notebook. Keep final copies in the classroom.

7. Lead Educator      Collect one final copy from every pupil and bind them in
[changed: CL-04]      order into a single class book with thread and reused
                      card, kept in the classroom. Include every pupil's tale
                      without ranking or marking a best. Each pupil may leave
                      their name off their page.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Family elder available to tell a tale; paper and pencils for
  sustained drafting across 3 weeks
  If not: If no elder is available, the pupil uses one textbook tale as the
  collected tale; if a week is lost, combine the two drafting lessons into one
  and shorten peer feedback to one spoken suggestion
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Connected Learning, CL-04 Collective Shared Purpose: bound every pupil's
  final tale into one classroom book as a shared product
