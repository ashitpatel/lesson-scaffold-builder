==============================================================================
LESSON SCAFFOLD: JHS 2 Social Studies - Section 4: Community Development and
Local Government
==============================================================================
Goal: Explain how a district governance body decides among community needs
with limited funds and make a reasoned case for a project.
Grade band: Middle. Period: 45 minutes.

UNIT                  WHAT IT IS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MLS-COMDEV-01         How the District Governance Body Works (2 weeks). Starts
                      fresh (no earlier unit needed).
MLS-COMDEV-02         Finding Our Community Needs (2 weeks). Starts fresh (no
                      earlier unit needed).
MLS-COMDEV-03         Costs and Choosing Within a Fixed Budget (3 weeks).
                      Builds on MLS-COMDEV-02.
MLS-COMDEV-04         Public Meeting and Letter to the Representative (3
                      weeks). Builds on MLS-COMDEV-01, MLS-COMDEV-03.

Units that build on the same earlier unit, or start fresh, can run at the same
time.

The LSB framework functions purely as an adaptive, supportive scaffolding
framework for lesson content delivery. It does not prescribe operational
classroom routines, behavior management habits, or physical school rules.


==============================================================================
MLS-COMDEV-01: How the District Governance Body Works
2 weeks. Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
==============================================================================
Goal: Builds the governance vocabulary of representative, committee, plan and
public report that the budget choice and letter depend on.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Explain the district governance body using chalk and
[changed: EB-05]      board. Name the elected Community_Representative, the
                      appointed members, the chief administrator, and the
                      Community_Committee that brings community needs. Draw a
                      simple diagram with the Community_Committee box joined
                      by a line to the body box and the development plan as a
                      list beside it. Say explicitly that the line shows needs
                      moving to the body and the list shows chosen projects,
                      keeping the names beside the drawing.

2. Lead Educator      Each pupil copies a three-row table into their notebook.
                      Rows list the Community_Representative, the
                      Community_Committee, and the development plan with one
                      line for what each does. Check notebooks for complete
                      rows.

3. Lead Educator      Show on chart paper how the body picks yearly projects
[changed: EB-04]      from the plan. Give one example choice between two
                      community needs. Then ask one question every pupil
                      answers at once on slates about which project was
                      chosen, look at all slates, and re-explain the choice if
                      many are wrong before pupils write it in notebooks.

4. Lead Educator      Ask pupils in turn to describe one role and the
                      development plan in one sentence. Correct errors on the
                      board. Pupils fix their notebook tables.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Chalk, board, notebooks and chart paper are available for two weeks.
  If not: If chart paper is missing, keep the roles table on the board and in
  notebooks only; if one week is lost, combine steps 3 and 4 into one period
  and drop repeated recitation.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-05 Concrete and Visual Representations: pairs the roles
  explanation with a simple box-and-line diagram on the board with the written
  names kept beside it and the link stated
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: adds one question every
  pupil answers at once on slates after the example choice, with the educator
  looking at all slates and re-explaining if many are wrong


==============================================================================
MLS-COMDEV-02: Finding Our Community Needs
2 weeks. Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
==============================================================================
Goal: Produces the ordered class needs chart that the budget work, vote and
letter use as evidence.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Peer Facilitator   In small groups, list needs of the community around the
[changed: CL-02]      school on reused paper. Use needs such as a water point,
                      a footbridge, street lamps, a clinic extension, a
                      library room, and a market shed. Within each group name
                      a Suggester, a Recorder and a Checker, swapping roles
                      halfway through the list. Keep all lists in the
                      classroom.

2. Lead Educator      Collect each group list on the board into one class
                      chart grouped by tap queue, latrine path, and dusty play
                      area. Count how many groups named each need and write
                      the class-level totals beside it. Keep the chart for
                      later units.

3. Lead Educator      Lead a class discussion on which needs are most urgent
                      and why, for example shade in hot wind season or tap
                      queues. Agree an urgency order and number the chart
                      items. Pupils copy the ordered chart into notebooks.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Classroom time for group listing and board space for one class chart.
  If not: If board space is short, keep the chart on joined reused paper; if
  one week is lost, combine listing and tallying in one period and shorten the
  urgency discussion to one round.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Connected Learning, CL-02 Structural Peer Roles: added rotating Suggester,
  Recorder and Checker roles to the group needs listing


==============================================================================
MLS-COMDEV-03: Costs and Choosing Within a Fixed Budget
3 weeks. Builds on MLS-COMDEV-02.
==============================================================================
Goal: Turns the needs chart into costed project sets that fit a fixed budget
and a voted first choice with reasons about fairness.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Recall from MLS-COMDEV-02: use the class chart of
[changed: EB-03]      ordered community needs. Explain the central development
                      fund gives 500000 monetary units for new projects, and
                      write the cost list on the board: water point 120000,
                      footbridge 200000, street lamps 90000, clinic extension
                      300000, library room 150000, market shed 80000. Work one
                      fitting set step by step on the board, adding two
                      project costs and comparing the total to the budget.
                      Leave the cost list on the board for group work.

2. Peer Facilitator   In mixed small groups, work out two different sets of
[changed: CL-02]      projects that fit within the budget. Assign Calculator,
                      Recorder and Checker within each group and rotate roles
                      for the second set. Show each addition sum in notebooks
                      using only numbers on paper. Keep all work in the
                      classroom with no real money.

3. Lead Educator      Ask each group to read out one fitting set with its
                      total. Record fitting sets on the board and correct any
                      sums. Pupils copy the corrected sets into notebooks.

4. Lead Educator      Hold a mock class vote on which project the governance
[changed: PB-03]      body should fund first. Announce both rules first:
                      single-choice means each pupil votes once and approval
                      means each pupil marks every option they accept on a
                      paper slip. Tally class-level totals for both methods on
                      the board and compare the winners. Write the winning
                      option, the rules used and that the top option feeds the
                      letters.

5. Lead Educator      Lead a discussion on whether the vote is fair to groups
                      whose project was not chosen, such as different blocks
                      or language groups sharing one grant. Ask pupils to give
                      one reason for and one reason against the result. Record
                      main points on the board.

6. Lead Educator      In week 3, open one lesson with four short questions on
[changed: EB-01]      budget sets and the vote, answered from memory on scrap
                      paper with the chart and notebooks put away. Show the
                      answers on the board straight after so each pupil checks
                      their own. Nothing is collected or marked.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Three weeks of class time with board and notebooks for sums, vote and
  discussion.
  If not: If one week is lost, use one fitting set per group instead of two
  and combine sharing with the vote in one period; drop the second discussion
  round.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-03 Worked Examples, then Practice: one complete fitting
  set is worked step by step on the board before groups solve their own sets
- Evidence-Based, EB-01 Retrieval Practice: low-stakes recall of budget sets
  and the vote from memory in week 3, self-checked
- Connected Learning, CL-02 Structural Peer Roles: added rotating Calculator,
  Recorder and Checker roles to the existing group budget-set work
- Participatory Budgeting, PB-03 Counting Methods: single-choice vote replaced
  by mock approval vote compared with single-choice count, with both rules
  announced first and result written on board


==============================================================================
MLS-COMDEV-04: Public Meeting and Letter to the Representative
3 weeks. Builds on MLS-COMDEV-01, MLS-COMDEV-03.
==============================================================================
Goal: Uses the governance roles and costed choices to practise public
questioning and to write and revise a reasoned project letter.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Recall from MLS-COMDEV-01 and MLS-COMDEV-03: use the
[changed: EB-09]      roles chart and the fitting budget sets. Ask why the
                      body reports spending at the public meeting, pupils
                      answer from the lesson, then state that it reports what
                      was done with the money so residents can ask questions.
                      Pupils write two questions they would ask in notebooks.

2. Lead Educator      Each pupil chooses one project for their own block, like
                      a tap shelter or shaded study corner, from the class
                      chart for their letter. Pupils write one line naming the
                      project and two reasons drawn from the needs list and
                      cost work. Keep names only on their own work.

3. Lead Educator      Give a full lesson of quiet writing time. Each pupil
                      drafts a one to two page letter to the
                      Community_Representative that names the project, gives
                      two reasons with evidence, and answers one objection.

4. Peer Facilitator   In pairs, read each other's draft against the class
                      needs chart and budget sets. Each partner writes one
                      note on what is missing or unclear, then partners swap
                      roles.

5. Lead Educator      Give a second full lesson for each pupil to revise the
                      draft using the partner note. Each pupil writes a final
                      copy in their notebook.

6. Lead Educator      Arrange a 10-minute session with another class in the
[changed: CL-05]      classroom, with the educator present. Pairs take turns
                      reading their final letters aloud in English, Kiswahili,
                      or a home language they write best, to a small group of
                      listeners. Reading is a choice: a student may lay the
                      letter on the desk for listeners to read, or keep it and
                      just listen.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Two full periods of quiet writing time within three weeks plus
  notebooks for drafts.
  If not: If time is lost, shorten the questions step to one question and
  combine choosing with drafting; protect one revision period and drop copying
  of the final copy if needed.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-09 Elaborative Interrogation: meeting explanation becomes
  asking why the body reports spending, pupils attempt from the lesson, then
  the precise answer is stated
- Connected Learning, CL-05 Real Audience Production: final letters read to
  another class as a real audience
