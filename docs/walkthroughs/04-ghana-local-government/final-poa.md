==============================================================================
LESSON SCAFFOLD: JHS 2 Social Studies - Section 4: Community Development and
Local Government
==============================================================================
Goal: Pupils explain how a district decides among community needs with limited
funds and make a reasoned case for a project.
Grade band: Middle. Period: 45 minutes.

UNIT                  WHAT IT IS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MLS-COMDEV-01         District Governance and Community Needs (2 weeks).
                      Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
MLS-COMDEV-02         Projects and Costs Within a Fixed Budget (2 weeks).
                      Builds on MLS-COMDEV-01.
MLS-COMDEV-03         Deciding Together and Recommending a Project (3 weeks).
                      Builds on MLS-COMDEV-02.

Units that build on the same earlier unit, or start fresh, can run at the same
time.

The LSB framework functions purely as an adaptive, supportive scaffolding
framework for lesson content delivery. It does not prescribe operational
classroom routines, behavior management habits, or physical school rules.


==============================================================================
MLS-COMDEV-01: District Governance and Community Needs
2 weeks. Starts fresh (no earlier unit needed).
==============================================================================
Goal: Builds the governance vocabulary and the class needs list that later
budgeting and recommendation work uses as evidence.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Explain the council system using chalk and board:
[changed: EB-04]      elected area representatives and appointed members led
                      by a council head decide, community committees bring
                      needs, and the council lists funded projects in a
                      development plan. Ask two questions every pupil answers
                      at the same time on slates or scrap paper held up, such
                      as who brings needs and what the plan lists. Look at all
                      answers and re-explain any part many miss before moving
                      on. Nothing is collected or marked.

2. Lead Educator      In small groups, list needs of the community around the
                      school such as water point, footbridge, lights, clinic,
                      library, and market shed near a praça in Restinga, an
                      arroio margin, or a bus stop. Write each list in
                      notebooks with pencil. Stay in the classroom for this
                      listing task.

3. Lead Educator      Ask each group to read its list aloud. Write all needs
                      on the board in one class list. Discuss together which
                      needs are most urgent and why.

4. Lead Educator      Take the whole class together for a short walk on school
[changed: CL-02]      grounds to observe needs, such as flooded arroio
                      margins, shadeless praça corners, and broken bus stops.
                      The educator stays with all the groups at all times. In
                      small groups with Spotter, Tally Keeper, and Reporter
                      roles that rotate each stop, pupils tally what they see
                      on chart paper as class totals only, with no names or
                      household details. They touch nothing, pick up nothing,
                      and lift nothing.

5. Lead Educator      In week 2, open a lesson with four short questions on
[changed: EB-01]      the council system and needs list, answered from memory
                      on scrap paper or slates with notebooks and charts put
                      away. Show the correct answers straight after so each
                      pupil checks their own. Nothing is collected or marked.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Dry weather and access to school grounds for a short supervised walk;
  chalk, board, chart paper available.
  If not: If the walk is not possible, skip the walk and use classroom lists
  only, combine listing and sharing in one period, and drop the walk tally
  within the same 2 weeks.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: every pupil answers two
  questions on slates at the same time, educator looks at all answers and
  re-explains before moving on
- Evidence-Based, EB-01 Retrieval Practice: week-2 low-stakes recall of
  council terms and needs from memory, self-checked, nothing collected
- Connected Learning, CL-02 Structural Peer Roles: added rotating Spotter,
  Tally Keeper, and Reporter roles to the school-grounds tally


==============================================================================
MLS-COMDEV-02: Projects and Costs Within a Fixed Budget
2 weeks. Builds on MLS-COMDEV-01.
==============================================================================
Goal: Teaches pupils to combine project costs within a fixed yearly fund so
choices reflect limited funds.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Recall from MLS-COMDEV-01: bring out the class list of
[changed: EB-04]      community needs. Explain the yearly district development
                      fund with one worked example on the board, saying each
                      adding step aloud. The example fund is 500000 monetary
                      units for new projects this year. Ask one question every
                      pupil answers at the same time on scrap paper held up,
                      look at all answers, and re-explain if many are wrong
                      before moving on, with nothing collected or marked.

2. Lead Educator      Give the cost list on the board: water point 120000,
[changed: CL-02]      footbridge 200000, streetlights 90000, clinic extension
                      300000, library room 150000, and market shed 80000
                      monetary units. In pairs, assign Adder and Checker: the
                      Adder adds a set and the Checker checks the total
                      against 500000, then they swap roles for the next set.
                      Each pair shows its sums in notebooks.

3. Lead Educator      Ask pairs to share one fitting set each. Record fitting
                      sets on chart paper with their totals. Check together
                      that no total passes 500000 monetary units.

4. Lead Educator      Close with a short written task where each pupil copies
                      two fitting sets and states what is left out. Use
                      notebooks only for this product. Create no extra logs or
                      forms.

5. Lead Educator      In week 2, open one lesson with three short questions on
[changed: EB-01]      project costs and the 500000 limit, answered from memory
                      on scrap paper with notebooks and chart put away. Show
                      the answers straight after so each pupil checks their
                      own. Nothing is collected, marked or recorded.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: Chalk and board or chart paper available for cost table and totals.
  If not: If materials are short, use reused paper or slates, record only two
  fitting sets, and combine sharing and written task in one period within the
  same 2 weeks.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: every pupil answers one
  question at the same time on scrap paper, educator looks and re-explains if
  many are wrong
- Evidence-Based, EB-01 Retrieval Practice: low-stakes week-2 recall of costs
  and limit from memory, self-checked
- Connected Learning, CL-02 Structural Peer Roles: pairs use rotating Adder
  and Checker roles for adding and checking sets


==============================================================================
MLS-COMDEV-03: Deciding Together and Recommending a Project
3 weeks. Builds on MLS-COMDEV-02.
==============================================================================
Goal: Turns the needs evidence and budget totals into a class decision and
each pupil's own reasoned project letter with revision.

WHO                   WHAT TO DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Lead Educator      Recall from MLS-COMDEV-02: use the chart of fitting
[changed: PB-03]      project sets and totals. As a mock exercise, announce
                      the rule first: each pupil votes for one project on a
                      paper slip as neighborhood delegates do in the city's
                      assembly cycle, then marks every option they accept.
                      Hold both counts on the board as class totals only and
                      compare the winners.

2. Lead Educator      Discuss whether the winning choice is fair to
                      communities that did not get their project from vilas
                      populares to wealthy bairros with paved streets. Use the
                      needs list and cost totals as evidence. Record two
                      fairness points on the board.

3. Lead Educator      Explain what happens at a public progress meeting: the
[changed: EB-04]      council reports what was done with the money and
                      residents ask questions. Ask every pupil to hold up one
                      finger for report and two for question on two examples,
                      look at all fingers and re-explain if many are wrong. If
                      the class can attend together, the whole class goes
                      together and the educator stays with all groups. They
                      touch nothing, pick up nothing, and lift nothing during
                      any visit.

4. Lead Educator      Give a full lesson of quiet writing time. Each pupil
                      drafts a one to two page letter to the elected area
                      representative recommending one project. The draft gives
                      at least two reasons with evidence from the needs list
                      and cost work and answers one objection.

5. Peer Facilitator   In pairs, read each other's draft and check it against
                      the needs evidence, reasons, and objection answer. Each
                      partner writes one note on what is missing or unclear,
                      then partners swap roles.

6. Lead Educator      Give a second full lesson for each pupil to revise the
                      draft using the partner's note and write a final copy in
                      their notebook. Create no extra reports beyond the
                      letters.

7. Lead Educator      Arrange a 10-minute session with another class in the
[changed: CL-05]      classroom, with the educator present. Pairs take turns
                      reading their final letters aloud to a small group of
                      listeners. Reading aloud is a choice: a student may lay
                      the letter on the desk for listeners to read, or keep it
                      and just listen.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
- Needs: A public progress meeting time is known and the elected
  representative can be reached through the school office.
  If not: If no meeting can be attended together, the educator explains the
  meeting on the board instead of a visit and letters stay in notebooks
  pending educator approval; combine vote and fairness discussion in one
  period within the same 3 weeks.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE
- Evidence-Based, EB-04 Checking for Understanding: added simultaneous finger
  check on report versus question parts with educator look and re-explanation
  before moving on
- Connected Learning, CL-05 Real Audience Production: final letters read to
  another class as a real audience
- Participatory Budgeting, PB-03 Counting Methods: single-choice vote compared
  with approval count on paper slips, rule announced first as mock exercise,
  both tallied as class totals only
